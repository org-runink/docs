The desktop

Using the desktop

The desktop is DEsk on Wayland: one wlroots compositor that is the whole desktop. Windows are sheets of paper on a desk, programs sit in a bookcase, notifications arrive as letters in a post box, and the screen locks at a front door. It is built in the [runink] repository from DEsk's release plus the Runink fork's patches (runink-desk). Nothing in the session is Python; the few jobs DEsk would hand to a Python program are done by small Go helpers instead.

Signing in

greetd shows the login screen, gtkgreet in the cage compositor, on the dark Runink River wallpaper. Sign in with the account the installer created. The same password unlocks your keyring: the login screen's PAM stack (/etc/pam.d/greetd) includes pam_gnome_keyring, so programs that keep passwords in the GNOME keyring (Wi-Fi passphrases from the network applet, for example) do not ask again.

Common tasks

Connect to a network

The network applet in the tray (nm-applet) handles wired, Wi-Fi and VPN connections, and nm-connection-editor edits them. From a terminal, nmcli and nmtui do the same: Networking.

Memory sticks and external disks

A memory stick or disk that udisks2 marks for automounting is mounted for you as you put it in, and any that are already in when you sign in. Each mount arrives as a letter in the post box. This is river-desk automount, started at login from /etc/xdg/autostart/river-automount.desktop.

Battery

On a laptop, river-desk battery sends a letter when a discharging battery falls to 15 %, and again at 5 %.

Bluetooth

Shell Settings' Bluetooth row (and DEsk's Bluetooth lever) opens Bluetooth, river-bluetooth: bluetuith, a terminal program for finding, pairing and connecting devices, in a foot window. bluetoothctl works too. The bluetoothd service runs under s6.

Printers

Shell Settings' Printers row opens Printers, river-printers: CUPS's own pages at http://localhost:631/ in your browser, where you add and manage printers. cupsd runs under s6. Printers on your LAN are found over mDNS, which the firewall lets in.

The weather at the front door

Upstream DEsk asks open-meteo.com for the weather every half hour. Runink River builds it with -Dweather_default=false, so nothing is asked until you tick "The real weather at the front door" in Shell Settings. Then one request goes out every 30 minutes, carrying only the latitude and longitude of the city your time zone is named after, made by DEsk itself. Off, the door still shows the hour and the porch lamp, and the weather is always fine.

Starting the desk over

DEsk keeps your settings, its traced room and its logs under ~/.config/desk, ~/.local/share/desk, ~/.local/state/desk and ~/.cache/desk. Log out and remove them to start with a fresh desk; your files are elsewhere. The first login after that traces the room again (a few seconds on a GPU, longer on a screen drawn by the CPU). runink-desk is part of the image and greetd starts it, so it is not removed with pacman -R.

What differs from upstream DEsk

The desk, drawer, bookcase, post box, Control Panel, camera, calculator, notebook, front door and the keys are upstream DEsk v0.2.1's code. What Runink River changes:

  • No Python. DEsk's own build runs none, and its Python helpers are replaced: automounting and the low-battery letter are river-desk (Go) rather than udiskie and poweralertd; Bluetooth is river-bluetooth (bluetuith) rather than blueman; printers are CUPS's own pages rather than system-config-printer. desk-look bake (which needs Blender) is not installed.
  • elogind, not systemd: sd-bus comes from libelogind, and the session does not ask a systemd user manager anything.
  • The weather is off until you turn it on (above).
  • One login screen (gtkgreet in cage, branded), no session chooser, since there is one desktop.
  • A smaller set of programs than upstream's installer brings: foot, Firefox, Thunar and Mousepad. Most of the rows on Shell Settings' System tab (sound, look, default programs, disks, date and time, users, passwords, packages, backups, task manager, about) open programs this image does not carry, so they show faint with the name of the package.
  • No power-profile switch or auto-rotate (power-profiles-daemon, iio-sensor-proxy: no s6 service yet) and no input-method framework for typing Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese or Thai into DEsk's own fields (IBus needs Python), and no Klee One (the memo pad falls back to another handwriting face). The screen-share chooser shares the first screen without asking (no yad).
  • Not yet proven on a booted image: the lamp-lit login, the front door's lock, the Control Panel's volume, brightness and radio levers, the post box and the camera under greetd and s6. Nobody has booted this build since DEsk replaced Plasma; the checklist is in docs/DESKTOP-DESK-PLAN.md.

Under the hood

The session runs on s6 through elogind and dbus, not systemd: s6 init. The helpers are one Go program, river-desk (installer/desk/, standard library only), shipped in runink-installer; it starts only the programs it names (foot, xdg-open, udisksctl, gdbus, notify-send). The design record for the move to DEsk is docs/DESKTOP-DESK-PLAN.md.
