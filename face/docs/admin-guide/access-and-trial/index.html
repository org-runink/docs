Access and the trial

Access and the trial

Who can sign in

FACE has no public sign-up. Only accounts Runink has approved can sign in, with an email and password or with Google single sign-on where your deployment offers it. Anyone else is refused at sign-in, and a public form only records a request to be contacted.

  • Access is granted by Runink. To add a person, ask your Runink contact. There is no invitation form, members list or role editor in the FACE app.
  • Administrators are added by Runink too. No page in FACE promotes anyone; the Admin page says so.
  • Roles. An approved person has one role: Viewer (reads), Editor (works with the data: asks, briefs, decisions, within what each card's approval rule allows) or Admin (also the Admin page: guard rules, external-search names, thresholds, standing rules and the decision log). The server checks the role on every call.
  • Suppliers. A supplier contact can be approved with the supplier role and one party. It sees only the orders addressed to that party, and every change it makes arrives as a proposal a named person approves. There is no guest link.

The trial

The trial lasts 14 days, then the workspace is read-only: nothing is deleted, and everything stays readable. Access to a trial is granted by Runink, like any other access. Trials are the same for everyone.

After the trial

Buying a plan lifts the read-only state. Plans and prices are on the FACE product page at runink.org; to buy one, contact Runink. Settings › Subscription shows the plan Runink recorded for your organisation. FACE takes no payment inside the app and does not charge by usage.

Availability

FACE is not generally available yet. The web app exists; the Android app is built but not published on Google Play; the iOS app is not built; there is no Google Cloud Marketplace listing. Runink FACE is sold separately from Runink TIDDE (Trusted Intelligence for Developer & Data Experience), Runink PULSE (Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine) and Runink LUNA (Lifestyle Understanding & Navigation Assistant), and the Runink platform layer it runs on ships with it.
