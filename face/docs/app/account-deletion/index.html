      Using FACE
      Delete your account

      Delete your account

      You can delete your own FACE account from inside the app. This page says where, what is deleted, what your organisation keeps, and how to ask for deletion if you can no longer use the app.

      In the app

      1. Open Settings (the foot of the rail), then the Account tab.
      2. In the Your data card, choose Delete my account.
      3. Read what is deleted and what is kept, then type your own email address in the field. The red Delete my account permanently button stays disabled until the text is your address.
      4. Choose Delete my account permanently. FACE deletes the account on the server first. Only when the server confirms does the app sign you out and clear the data it holds on your device. If the server cannot be reached, or refuses, the dialog says so and your account is not deleted.

      The deletion is always of the account you are signed in with. The server takes it from your verified session, never from what the request says, and checks the address you typed against that session again.

      The only admin cannot delete the account. If you are the last organisation admin of the instance, FACE refuses and says so: make another member an admin first, then delete. The instance’s break-glass administrator account cannot be deleted from the app.

      What is deleted

      • your sign-in account: email address, password hash, two-factor secret and role;
      • every session of yours, on every device, which end at once;
      • the follow-up flows you own, with their runs, their chat and the proposals made for you;
      • the scenarios, items and terms you typed into the scenario builder;
      • the mailbox connections you started and did not finish;
      • the brief suggestions you dismissed;
      • on your device: the cached data and your saved role.

      You are signed out on every device first, then your data is deleted, then the account record. Earlier encrypted versions of these records stay in FACE’s storage history until it is compacted and cleaned, which is scheduled for about a day later; nobody can read them without FACE’s key. A personal brief you saved keeps a code derived from your account and is not deleted yet.

      What your organisation keeps

      What belongs to your organisation is not yours personally and stays: briefs, personas, connections, schedules, the run history, the mailbox your organisation connected, the follow-up tasks flows created, the audit log and the decision records.

      You are not named in them afterwards. Where a retained record names you (a task you finished, a persona you edited, a decision you made), it shows a fixed code, former-member- and ten characters, instead: the same code for the same person every time. The decision log is a hash chain and is not rewritten: its stored entries keep an anonymous account number that no longer links to any account or email address, and the code is shown where the log is read.

      One audit entry records that the deletion happened: when, which classes of data, and a hash standing for the account. It holds no content and no address.

      Not reached by the in-app deletion

      An older copy of FACE’s sign-in table, from before FACE moved its storage, is keyed by your email address and is not reached by the in-app deletion. An operator removes it on request: write to privacy@runink.org from your sign-in address.

      After the deletion: the access list is not edited

      FACE’s access list is built from add-users requests that FACE itself cannot change. Deleting your account does not remove your address from it. If your address is still listed, signing in with Google again creates a new, empty account. To stop that, ask your organisation’s administrator to remove you from the list.

      Without the app

      If you can no longer open the app, write to privacy@runink.org from the address you sign in with, with the subject Delete my FACE account. The same page is published for the app stores at runink.org/account-deletion.

      To ask for a copy of your data first, write to the same address with the subject Export my FACE data: there is no in-app export. The app offers this as a link in the delete dialog.

      Retrying

      Deleting is safe to repeat. If a call fails part-way, you are already signed out, but the account record goes last, so it is still there: sign in again and repeat the deletion. A repeat after success deletes nothing more and writes no second audit entry.
