Accessibility and languages

Accessibility and languages

FACE works with a keyboard and a screen reader, never relies on colour alone, comes in light and dark themes, and speaks English, French, Spanish and Portuguese.

Screen readers and the keyboard

  • Navigation. Each rail entry is a real button. It takes keyboard focus, shows a visible focus ring, and responds to Enter and Space. Screen readers announce its label and whether it is the page you are on.
  • Icon-only buttons. A button without a text label carries a tooltip and a spoken label. For example, the assistant button is announced as Open FACE AI assistant.
  • Status changes. Errors and changing states are announced as they happen, such as the filter notice on Actionable Twins, a list that failed to load, the reject dialog's character counter, the answer on Ask, and the hand-off to the twins on Options.
  • The map. On the Map view, On the map lists every placed thing, so a keyboard can open the same card a tap does.
  • Reduced motion. When your system asks for reduced motion, FACE stops decorative animation, such as the rotation of partner messages on the activity card.

Severity is never colour alone

Neighbouring severity colours are too close to tell apart by colour alone, so every severity mark also has its own shape, and usually its word:

Severity Glyph
Critical filled circle with an exclamation mark
High warning triangle
Medium square
Low dash
OK check mark

Other states work the same way:

  • Rule verdicts on Why pair a glyph with the words Aligned, Drift, Shadow, Missing and Unrated.
  • How a scenario ended on Options is an icon and a word.
  • Remediation banners and audit rows print their status word beside the glyph.

Light and dark themes

FACE has separate light and dark themes, each designed for contrast in its own right. Choose under Settings › Account, next to Theme: Light, Dark or System, which follows your device. This device remembers your choice.

Languages

FACE is available in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. Under Settings › Account:

  • Interface language sets the language of the app's screens. The interface switches at once.
  • Agent language sets the language the agent listens and reads its answers aloud in, and the language FACE writes its answers in.

Some text is not translated: technical identifiers, and text that comes word for word from a server or a source system. FACE marks where a server's own reply is shown.
