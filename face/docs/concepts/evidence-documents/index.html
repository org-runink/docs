Evidence documents

Evidence documents

An evidence document (EvidenceDocument) is a document a third party acts on, such as an adjuster, a carrier's claims desk or a court. FACE attaches one to an ActionCard in evidence_document. You read it on the card on Actionable Twins, and in full through the card's View full decision artifact. It is sealed when a person approves the card. The contract is strict about what these documents are and are not:

These documents assemble a file, show what was read, and propose one next step for a person entitled to take it. They do not underwrite, bind, settle, or satisfy an obligation on anyone's behalf.

What a document holds

Every figure in a document cites the record it came from. narrative is optional prose that may be drafted for a person to read.

Field What it holds
kind One value from EvidenceDocumentKind (below).
subject_id The customer's own identifier for the shipment, claim or entry.
facts (DocumentFact) What the records say. Every fact carries at least one citation id.
findings (DocumentFinding) What was determined, as stable codes such as OSD_SHORT or SLA_BREACH_CONFIRMED, with a severity and the figures used.
citations (Citation) Where each figure came from (see below).
deadlines (DocumentDeadline) A date computed from a record date by a named rule (for example CARMACK_NOTICE_9M, COGSA_SUIT_1Y) with its authority, and the citation that supplied the start date.
claimed_amount, exposure_amount, units_short, units_damaged Headline figures. Absent means not measured.
absent (AbsentFigure) Why a figure is missing, for example NO_BACKING_RECORD, NOT_STATED_ON_DOCUMENT or ILLEGIBLE.
narrative (DocumentNarrative) Optional prose. authored_by names the author, and figure_ids lists every figure the prose mentions.
seal (DocumentSeal) Set at approval. Never set at derivation.

Citations

A Citation has everything a reader needs to go and check a figure:

  • source_kind, source_id (the record's id in the customer's system), and the field read.
  • value: the value as read.
  • connection_id, plus lineage_record_id pointing at the extraction record.
  • observed_at: when the record was read.
  • perception: set when a value was read off a scanned document image. If it is absent, the value came from a structured record.

Sealing

A document is sealed when an operator approves the card. The seal (seal) shows that the document has not changed since approval. A rejected card is left unsealed.

The vocabulary: 18 document kinds

EvidenceDocumentKind is a fixed set of kinds. FACE emits the 18 kinds below.

The kind names above drop the EVIDENCE_DOCUMENT_KIND_ prefix. See Action cards & money for how money is shown.

Names chosen to avoid over-claiming

EQUIPMENT_LIMIT_APPROACH is deliberately not called "predictive maintenance". The deriver measures a channel's trend against a limit the feed itself states, and reports when that trend reaches the limit. No failure probability is produced.

CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_AUDIT keeps one rule: an unrecorded handoff is not a break in custody. Every finding is a statement about the record, never about the goods or the people handling them.

The underwriting documents never decide coverage. No finding code says VOID, DECLINED, REPUDIATED or anything like it. Those are an underwriter's words.

Reading the results

  • A document is a file, not a decision. It proposes one next step for a person entitled to take it. It doesn't serve notice, file a claim or settle anything.
  • A computed deadline is an input, not legal advice. It doesn't preserve a right on anyone's behalf. The document carries due_unix and no "days remaining" figure; the client computes the countdown at render time.
  • A citation proves provenance, not correctness. It shows exactly which record a figure came from. It doesn't show that the record was right.
  • A seal shows the document has not changed since approval. It doesn't prove who approved them. For that, see the decision trail on Action cards & money.
