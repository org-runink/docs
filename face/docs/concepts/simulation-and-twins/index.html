Simulation & twins

Simulation & twins

After a run answers your question on Ask, two pages carry it forward:

  • Options answers "what could we do, and what is each option worth?". It leads with the options for the decision behind the run's checks, scored and sendable for approval. Below them is what FACE tested: the run's scenarios as a list, with a Graph view, and a New Simulation action. The status counts, what went to the twins and the run step by step sit in the folded How FACE knows this.
  • Actionable Twins holds the decision cards in three lanes (Waiting for you, In flight, Back), with a Map view on the same page that places each card where your own data says it is.

This page covers the services behind both, and the claims and reverse-logistics service.

Options and scenarios (HypothesisService)

RPC What it does Where you see it
CompareOptions Scores the what-if options for one decision on one shared measure over a run's measured rows, each with a 95% range, and names a recommended one only when it is the clear winner. Options, at the top
SendToTwins With decision_key, option_key and run_id: compares the options again and queues the named one for approval. Without them: turns a validated scenario into an action card. The card waits on Actionable Twins
GetHypothesisVariables Scenario groups inferred from the estate, with each scenario's simulation_state and the run-level swarm_census. Options
InteractiveSimulate / SimulateScenario Runs one scenario through the agent loop and returns SimulateScenarioResponse. The app uses InteractiveSimulate. Options
ValidateHypothesis Checks a scenario against the recognised rules and data. Options
GetTwinsHandoff / SetTwinsHandoffExclusion The scenarios a run's analysis handed to the twins agent, with each verdict and impact, and whether a person removed one from the next twins analyses. Options, How FACE knows this
GetScenarioBuilder, SaveCustomScenario, AddScenarioItem, AddScenarioTerm One run's scenarios with their business categories, the items each tests and the market terms each watches, plus what your team added. Options, New Simulation
AnalyzeTelemetry Agentic analysis of an equipment time series. The FACE app does not call it. API only

The scenario list and its Graph view read FetchService.GetScenarioGraph: the run's scenarios and what each one tested, computed from the run's stored records. Anything the run did not record is shown as a gap.

Comparing options

How the options are scored, recommended and sent for approval is covered in Action cards & money. In short: options are compared on one measure, and nothing runs until a named person approves.

Scenarios

FACE proposes scenarios from your estate's domains, the relationships it measured between them and your rules, and tests them. Options shows how many were tested and how many produced a result. A scenario that was not tested says so; it is never hidden. Each tested scenario returns its impacts (metric, change, risk level and horizon), the evidence it relied on, forecasts with intervals and any anomalies. Results are listed side by side.

Validation

ValidateHypothesis checks a scenario against your rules. valid_data says whether a fetch has grounded the instance, valid_logic says whether the scenario holds against your rules, each concern becomes a risk_flags entry, and rationale explains the verdict.

Digital twins (TwinsService)

RPC What it does Where you see it
GetActionQueue / ExecuteAction The action queue and the human decision. See Action cards & money. Actionable Twins lanes, and each card's place on the Map view
GetValueLedger Money identified, approved, filed, recovered and expired for a period, each figure with its basis. Actionable Twins (above the lanes) and the money line on Why
ListDecisionLog The decision log. Admins only. Settings › Account, Admin
ProposeStandingRule, ApproveStandingRule, RevokeStandingRule, ListStandingRules Turn an approved decision into a decision-authority rule. Proposing needs a writer; approving needs an org admin who is not the proposer. A decided card on Actionable Twins
ListAssetLocations Where each asset in FACE's fetched data is: a position only where a source stated one, and every other asset listed with the reason it has none. Map view
ListRunRoutes The routes and destinations one run inferred from the rows it read, each proposal with its judging state, and what is missing when no destination has a position. Map view
ListLiveTraffic / AnalyseRoutesLive Public transit and road feeds found from the run's towns and regions, read only while someone watches the map, and each route against the live events near it. Map view
ListRunProviders Service providers one run looked for on the public web near the sites in trouble (disposal, alternate warehousing, fleet repair, cold storage), with the queries sent. Map view
OptimizeRoute Computes a route through the session's Google Routes connector and returns distance, duration and polyline. Map view, fleet plan
StreamTwins, QuickAsk, UploadAnalyze Interactive twin reasoning over text, audio, frames or a document; retrieval-based Q&A for S&OP and inventory questions; root-cause or savings analysis of an uploaded CSV, Excel or PDF file. The query is screened before it reaches a model. The assistant, opened from its floating button on every page
GetSavingsSummary Pipeline savings and approved savings. The FACE app does not display it. API only
GenerateRoadmap A phased roadmap (Digitize, Connect, Analyze, Automate) from maturity scores. The FACE app does not call it. API only
SubmitFeedback Records feedback on a generated response to the durable record store.

From Actionable Twins you also open:

  • Follow-ups, which run on the cards selected on the list or the map.
  • The Compliance, Finance and Operations remediation views, from the page's Remediation links. See Compliance, finance, operations.
  • The site plans and the twin entities, as panels over the Map view.

Refusals are errors, not empty cards.

  • OptimizeRoute returns Unavailable when there's no routing connector or no route, rather than a card with blank measurements. cost_savings is left empty, because the routing provider returns no cost.
  • GenerateRoadmap leaves estimated_investment_usd and expected_roi_percent at zero, which means "not estimated": the roadmap describes a plan, not a measurement.

Claims and reverse logistics (ClaimsAgentService)

TriageReturnItem is a fixed rule over the condition grade the caller states:

condition_code Disposition
PRISTINE / A RESTOCK
DAMAGED / B REFURBISH
DEFECTIVE / C RECYCLE
EXPIRED / D LANDFILL
  • A missing return_id or condition_code, an unknown grade, or a negative value is refused with InvalidArgument. An unknown grade is never a decision to destroy stock.
  • estimated_recovery_yield_usd, refurbishment_cost_usd and routing_destination aren't derived, because FACE holds no pricing, cost or facility data for returns. basis_note says so on every response.

Reverse-logistics outcomes that rest on records are produced as RETURN_DISPOSITION evidence documents. See Evidence documents.

Reading the results

  • A simulation is a model's reasoning over grounded context. It isn't a measured outcome, and its confidence is the model's own.
  • The results cover only the scenarios that ran. Read the counts before treating the results as covering the estate.
  • Passing validation means the rules are consistent. It doesn't mean the scenario will happen.
  • A compared option is scored over the rows the run read. A recommendation means its range cleared the others on that sample, not that it will work.
  • A triage disposition follows the stated grade, not an inspection. It's only as good as the grade the caller supplied.
