Concepts

Concepts

FACE is multi-agent analysis over your own logistics data: it fetches from the systems you connect, extracts and profiles what it read, reasons over it with agents bounded by guardrails, with every proposed action independently reviewed, and recommends decisions that a named person approves before anything happens. This section explains the ideas behind those steps; the Architecture section shows the components.

Ideas that shape everything

  • Your own data, read-only. FACE reads the systems you connect and never writes to them to suit itself.
  • Sovereign inference. Every model call goes to an inference plane the deployment configures. FACE calls no outside AI service and links no AI vendor's SDK.
  • Agents draft, people decide. Nothing an agent proposes is sent, filed or paid until a named person approves it, and every decision is recorded on a log that admins can read.
  • Honest absence. A figure FACE did not measure is shown as Not measured.

Vocabulary

The terms below are used throughout the app, the API and these docs, in the order you meet them on the journey.

Term Meaning
Instance One FACE deployment for one organisation. Each customer gets its own instance, and only accounts Runink has approved for it can sign in.
Connection A configured link to one data source: its type (for example Snowflake, SAP, MQTT or a camera) and its settings. Credentials are stored separately and are write-only. See Data sources.
Runner The process that reads a data source. The managed runner is part of the server. Self-hosted runners run in your network and enrol with the instance.
Test A connection check. It opens the source with its stored credential, read-only and time-limited, and reads nothing. A connector with no reachability check says so.
Source backing Whether anything FACE ships stands behind a connection. LIVE means nothing of FACE's stands in, so the connection reads whatever it addresses and a failure surfaces as a failure. Unspecified means the server did not compute it.
Run One question asked of your data, on Ask or by a brief: FACE reads the sources, answers, then runs the analysis and the twins' decisions in the background. The app calls it a fetch.
Selected run The one run that Ask, Options, Why and Actionable Twins show. Pages never borrow figures from another run.
Persona Who a run or a brief is for: a name, the question its brief asks, the money figure it reads first, and its plays. The product ships five; an instance can reword them or add its own. With none picked, a run uses the Generalist view.
Play What FACE recovers or protects money on: claims recovery, disruptions, routes, fulfilment, cold chain, maintenance, inventory and fleet.
Brief A question FACE asks on its own, at a set time or when data changes, for one persona, telling the people you choose what needs a decision. See Briefs.
Option One way to act on a decision, scored on one shared measure with a range. FACE recommends an option only when it is a clear winner. See Options.
Check A test FACE runs on the rows a run read, such as "Deliveries arrive by their promise". It holds, fails, or is not measured. See Why.
Rule A business rule FACE found in your systems or you added, checked against what your systems do and against your policy documents.
Decision A drafted action (a claim, a dispute, an order, a route re-plan) waiting on Actionable Twins until a named person approves or rejects it. Lanes sort decisions into Waiting for you, In flight and Back.
Evidence document A structured document FACE derives from your data, such as an OS&D report, a temperature-excursion record or a custody audit. The set of document kinds is fixed, and each is built only from the records it cites.
Decision artifact The full record of one decision, with the drafts it would produce.
Follow-up A flow that calls or messages the people responsible for an item, on rules you approve. It runs by itself only once armed.
Remediation The work after a compliance, finance or operations finding: assign it, decide it and route it.
Trace The recorded history of a run: its steps, the sources it read, and what it produced.
Data lineage An append-only record of which source was read, when, with what shape and outcome. It records structure only, never the values returned.
Grounding Retrieving your own records (searching your own indexed records) to support a model's reasoning, so answers cite your data.

How results are produced

  • Fetch pipeline: What runs when you ask on Ask: phases, traces, SOPs, suggested questions and briefs.
  • Agents & oversight: The agents, their guardrails, the review of proposed actions and the human decision.
  • Grounding: Searching your own corpus so answers cite your data.
  • Sovereign models: The model plane FACE calls, and which model answered.
  • Structured output: How model answers become typed results, or are refused.
  • Evidence documents: The closed set of document kinds, each built from cited records.
  • Action cards & money: The cards on Actionable Twins, the money line and the decision trail.
  • Statistics & ML: The statistics and models behind the analysis.
  • Posture & maturity: Health scores, the domain graph and causal analysis of the estate.
  • Simulation & twins: Options, scenarios, digital twins, claims and returns.
  • Twin ontology & graph: The classes, relations and identifiers of the twin graph.
  • Compliance, finance, operations: Findings, remediation orders and routing.
  • Vision & CCTV: Frame grading, detection and coordinates.

Reading the results

Every page here ends with a Reading the results note: what a result shows, and what stays with the person who acts on it. FACE puts the difference between "measured zero" and "not measured" in the data itself, so a client that shows a number should read its companion reason first.
