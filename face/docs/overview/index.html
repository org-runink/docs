Overview

Overview

FACE by Runink (Fulfilment Autonomous Claims Engine) takes the chasing out of logistics claims and exceptions. Today a shortage, a warm reefer or an overbilled accessorial is found by someone reading across the warehouse system, the telematics feed and the paperwork, often after the claim window has closed. FACE connects to the systems an operation already runs (warehouses, ERPs, telematics, sensors, cameras), finds those cases, and hands a person the answer, the evidence and the drafted decision to approve.

Who uses it: operations, finance and claims teams in logistics, fulfilment and field operations, and the admins who connect their systems.

The models FACE reasons with are served by an inference plane the deployment configures: Runink's machines on a trial or when Runink runs FACE for you, your own servers when you host it. FACE calls no third-party AI service.

Availability

Note: FACE is not generally available yet. What exists today:

  • Built: the web app (the cockpit these pages describe), the gRPC API, the data connectors and the agents.
  • Not yet available: the Android app is built but not published on Google Play; the iOS app is not built; there is no Google Cloud Marketplace listing; there are no published customer results.
  • Access: by approval only. A trial lasts 14 days, then the workspace is read-only; access is granted by Runink. See Access and the trial.

Runink FACE is sold separately from Runink's other products: Runink TIDDE (Trusted Intelligence for Developer & Data Experience), Runink PULSE (Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine) and Runink LUNA (Lifestyle Understanding & Navigation Assistant). Plans and prices are on the product page at runink.org.
