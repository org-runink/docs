      Using the cockpit
      Twins

      Twins

      Twins is where FACE’s decisions are acted on. The digital twins propose actions, and nothing goes out to a third party until a person approves it. The TWINS section of the rail has two entries:

      • Map, the Twins landing page: your assets where they are, with their actionable cards on the map.
      • Today’s command: the same decisions as a list of orders, each with its items.

      Old links to the twins page open Today’s command.

      Map

      Map is a cozy, low-poly 3-D map. It draws your assets as extruded blocks at the physical location your data sources state, with the twins’ decisions on them. Open it with Map under TWINS in the rail (/twins/map).

      What the map draws

      • Placed assets: an asset is placed only where its source stated a position, such as a GPS fix. FACE geocodes nothing by guesswork.
      • Route proposals: a route is drawn in its proposed stop order. The line joins this card’s stated positions in the proposed order. It is not a road. Tap a route to see its stops, the basis of its distance and its decision.
      • Live connection layers: one layer per active connection that reports positions. A connection switched off on Fetch has no layer.
      • Site plans: the footprints of your site plans, from BIM/CAD plan files (IFC or DXF), for physical-storage awareness. Tap one to open the plan over the map. See Site plans.
      • Entities: the twin entities discovered in your data. See Entities.

      The category cards above the map (COMPLIANCE, FINANCE, OPERATIONS, S&OP, SAVINGS, ESG, PROCUREMENT and Market & diligence) filter the cards shown, exactly as on Today’s command. Tap the selected one again to show everything.

      Tap a place

      Tap an asset, an entity or a live feature. Its details open over the map: where its position came from (for example GPS fix · {time} · {source}), and its pending actionable cards. Each card is the same card as on Today’s command, with its own Approve and Reject. Nothing pending here means no card is about that place. Open in queue opens it on Today’s command.

      Not on the map lists everything the data names but the map cannot place, each with its reason: No location in the data, Address only — waiting for the FACE geocoder, Names more than one asset — not guessed, or Not about a particular asset.

      The layer panel

      Layers lists FACE’s own layers, then one layer per active connection. Each has a switch, how many features it has on the map ({count} on the map), the attribution its source requires, and how old its data is (Old data · newest {time}). A layer that cannot draw says why. Live layers could not be read: {reason} means the request failed.

      Basemap

      The map is keyless by default: FACE calls no third-party tile service. Without a basemap, it draws its own pastel ground, and every position is the one the data states (No basemap installed. FACE draws its own ground; every position is the one the data states.). If your deployment configures its own basemap, it is drawn under the map.

      Savings summary

      The SAVINGS SUMMARY floats over the map. This is the only place it appears. It shows the Total Identified Savings (USD) and a breakdown by category, and it tells four cases apart:

      You seeMeaning
      Measuring…Still loading.
      Not availableThe request failed. Nothing shown is a measurement.
      Not measuredNo summary has been requested yet.
      No savings identified in the current action setMeasured, and the result was nothing.

      The fetched data has no assets yet. and No data source is connected on this instance, so there is nothing to place. say why the map is empty. The map could not be loaded: {reason} means the request failed.

      Today’s command

      Today’s command lists every actionable twin as an order, with its items as SKUs. Open it with Today’s command under TWINS in the rail (/today_command).

      Filter the orders

      The chips under ACTIONABLE TWIN STATUS are All, COMPLIANCE, FINANCE, OPERATIONS, S&OP, SAVINGS, ESG and PROCUREMENT. Tap a chip to ask the server for that domain only. A selected chip shows a check mark as well as its colour.

      While a filter is on, a notice titled Filtered to {filter} explains that orders filed under another chip are not in the list. Show all removes the filter.

      COMPLIANCE, FINANCE and OPERATIONS each have a chevron button (tooltip Open COMPLIANCE, and so on). The chevron opens that twin’s remediation screen.

      Refresh reads the orders again. While a fetch is running, the page also shows Updating with the latest results… and reads the orders again by itself until the fetch ends.

      Read an order

      Each order is one decision. Its summary line shows the order’s status (Awaiting decision until someone decides), who it is with (With {name}), and its Total or Savings where the card states an amount of money. A priority chip prints the priority word (or NORMAL), and the order says which stage and run produced it, for example RECONCILE, FETCH or SIMULATE.

      Under it, Items ({count}) lists the line items, with the columns ITEM, QTY, STATUS and OWNER / SUPPLIER. The items are what a supplier or crew would act on: the stops of a route, the documents a customs hold is missing, the vendors a request for proposal recommends, the units short or damaged. A quantity appears only where the order states one; otherwise it reads not stated. No line items are stated on this order. means the order names none.

      Tap the order to expand it and see its artifacts: the email draft (TO:), the calendar invite and the ERP change (ERP: / Action:). View full decision artifact opens the decision artifact. Review Action (pencil) opens a dialog that summarises the decision, with Cancel, Reject and Approve. Reject Action — records a decision (✕) starts a rejection.

      Orders are grouped by deadline: Dated — soonest first, Date not established and No deadline applies.

      Approve an order

      Expand the order

      Tap the order and read its line items and the artifacts it will produce.

      Select Approve

      The order leaves the list at once. The decision is queued on your device and sent to the server as soon as there is a connection, so an approval made offline is not lost.

      Read the outcome

      If the order has artifacts, the cockpit opens its decision artifact in the approved state. Otherwise it shows Action approved — queued for execution.

      When the server records an approval but a part of it could not run, a notice titled Recorded, but not executed lists each part with its reason. For example, an email is not sent when no Google connector is configured for the account.

      Reject an order

      Every rejection asks for a reason first, because the reason is the only record of why the order was refused.

      Select Reject or the ✕ button

      The dialog Why are you rejecting this? opens and names the order.

      Write your reason

      Type it in Your reason. The counter below the field shows how much room is left. An empty reason is refused, and so is a reason too long to keep word for word. Long text is never cut short.

      Select Reject with this reason

      The order leaves the list and the rejection is kept in its decision record. If you change your mind later, the new decision is added to that record. It doesn’t replace the old one. Cancel leaves the order where it was.

      Analyze a document

      The upload button (Analyze document) sends a csv, txt, tsv or json file of up to 3 MB to the twins. Each agent’s progress shows in a dialog (Waiting for this agent…, Running, Reported), and the orders are read again when you close it.

      Remediation orders

      Each compliance finding can become a work order: the procedure, the specifications it must meet, and who it is addressed to. FACE sends no order anywhere. Approving records a decision, and then a person sends the order and marks it sent. Orders are grouped under WAITING FOR A PERSON and DECISION ON RECORD.

      On an order card:

      • NOTE is your reason in your own words. A rejection needs one.
      • CHANNEL is how you sent it (email, ticket, phone, and so on).
      • Assign opens Address this remediation, with the fields DEPARTMENT, SERVICE PROVIDER and CONTACT, and the buttons Save assignment and Cancel.
      • Approve and Reject record a decision.
      • Mark sent — records that you sent it; FACE sends nothing records that you sent it.

      An order’s state chip reads DRAFT, APPROVED, REJECTED or SENT. An order with no decision yet shows NOBODY HAS DECIDED, and one with no recipient shows ADDRESSED TO NOBODY YET.

      The orders section tells a failed request apart from an empty answer:

      HeadingMeaning
      ANALYSINGA compliance analysis is running.
      THE ANALYSIS FAILEDThe request failed. This is not an estate with nothing to do.
      NOTHING HAS BEEN ASKED YETNo compliance analysis has run here.
      NO ORDERS WERE DERIVEDEither no finding came back, or none could be turned into a procedure. Neither means the estate is clear.

      What the states mean

      You seeMeaning
      Digital twin reasoning…The orders are loading.
      Failed to fetch action queue: {error}The cockpit could not read the orders. This is not an empty list.
      No priority actionsThe twins have nothing awaiting your review. New orders appear as fetches and simulations complete.
      NOTHING WAS REPORTED (Findings)The assessment returned no findings. That describes the response. It is not a verdict that the estate is clean.
      Nothing is connected yetNo data source is attached to the instance. The instance’s own reply is shown under What this instance replied, in English:, and Connect a data source opens the connect wizard.

      Findings and measures

      Findings lists what the posture assessment reported, worst first. Measures to take lists the recommended measures that answer them, each marked with where it came from. Go to the measure, on a finding, scrolls to its measure.

      The rest of the page

      Below the orders, the page keeps its briefing: the command bar (Ask or command…, with Video for Surveillance and Voice chat for a voice call), the system posture, the ranked risks and opportunities, and the decisions you approved. What evidence did you use? opens the evidence behind them.

      RPCs behind these pages

      • TwinsService.GetActionQueue, ExecuteAction (sent from the offline queue), UploadAnalyze (server stream), GetSavingsSummary, ListAssetLocations.
      • MapFeatureService.ListMapLayers, StreamMapFeatures (server stream).
      • PostureService.GetHealthScore.
      • ComplianceService.AnalyzeCompliance, AssignRemediation, DecideRemediation.

      See the API reference.
