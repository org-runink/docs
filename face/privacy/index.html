FACE app privacy policy

English · Français · Español · Português

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the FACE Android app (to be distributed through Google Play; not yet published) by Technologies Runink Inc. (application id org.runink.face), and the FACE deployment it connects to, face.runink.org. FACE is a business console for logistics and fulfilment teams. The Runink privacy policy covers your organisation's account, its billing and the operational data FACE processes for it. This page covers what the app adds.

Who can use FACE

FACE has no public sign-up. An administrator of your organisation adds your email address to its FACE instance. Google sign-in works only for addresses on that list, and only when Google reports the address as verified. The first time you sign in, FACE creates an account for you with the role the administrator chose.

What FACE collects

Your account

  • Your email address. With Google sign-in, the app asks Google for openid, email and profile, and sends only Google's signed identity token to FACE. We never see your Google password.
  • Your account record holds an internal id, your email address, a password hash, your two-factor secret if you set one up, your role and the date the account was created. It holds no name.
  • Your session lasts 12 hours. Signing out ends it on the server first, and then removes the token from the phone.
  • Server logs record sign-in events, including the email address that signed in or was refused.

Questions and files

  • Questions you type or speak on Ask are kept with their run, so that the run's history can show them.
  • Files you attach are sent byte for byte, including any metadata they carry (for example, GPS tags in a photo taken by another camera app). FACE reads each file in memory and keeps the text it read, up to 64 KB per file, not the file itself.
  • Site plans are different: a plan you upload is stored as the original file.

Camera

The camera is used on one screen, and only while that screen is open.

  • Live detection. While the screen is open, frames go to your FACE deployment so that it can draw boxes and labels over the preview. These frames are not stored.
  • Grade. A photo is sent for analysis only when you press Grade. FACE stores the result (what was recognised, its material and condition), not the photo.
  • Evidence images. An instance's operator can choose to keep an annotated copy of a graded photo. This is off unless the operator turns it on. When it is on, a copy is kept only if grading found an object to inspect, and it is shown for at most 30 days. FACE's storage cannot delete what it wrote, so the encrypted file stays until the operator expires it.
  • Video clips are written to a temporary file to sample frames, and the file is deleted when sampling ends. FACE keeps the text it read from the clip.
  • People. FACE does not identify, track or profile people, and no crop of a person is kept. When you press Grade, the whole photo is analysed, so a person in it may be seen by the model. A retained evidence image is not blurred and can show a bystander.

Microphone and voice

The microphone is used only when you tap a microphone button. There is no background listening and no wake word.

  • Command bar and Fetch Center. Your voice goes to your FACE deployment, where FACE's own speech model turns it into text. The audio is not stored. The voice audit records sizes, never words. The text becomes your question.
  • Assistant panel. This microphone uses your phone's own speech recogniser. The command bar also uses it when FACE's speech service cannot be reached. Your voice then goes to that recogniser (on most Android phones, Google's, under Google's terms), and only the text reaches FACE.
  • Spoken replies are made by FACE's own voice and cached on the deployment, unencrypted, with no expiry. When FACE's voice cannot be reached, your phone's text-to-speech engine reads the reply.

On your phone

  • Your session token is kept in Android's Keystore-backed secure storage, and removed when you sign out.
  • A local cache of recent runs and queued changes is kept in the app's private storage, unencrypted. Signing out clears it (only the language setting is kept). It is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

What FACE does not collect

  • No location. The app does not ask for location permission. The map shows your organisation's assets from your organisation's own data, and FACE serves the map itself.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS, call log or photo library. Files reach FACE only when you pick them.
  • No purchases in the app from Google Play.

The app's Android permissions are: camera, microphone, audio settings (for a Bluetooth headset), internet and network state.

Who receives your data

The app talks to one server: your organisation's FACE deployment, operated by Runink. FACE's language, vision and speech models run on machines Runink operates, or on your organisation's own machines where its plan says so. No outside AI provider receives your photos, voice or questions.

The app itself also contacts:

  • Google, for sign-in through Google Play services, only if you choose Google sign-in;
  • your phone's speech recogniser and text-to-speech engine, in the cases above;
  • a map app on your phone, only when you choose to open a destination in it.

For your organisation, the deployment may also send search queries to public web search engines during an analysis, and send a result you choose to share by WhatsApp, SMS or voice call through your organisation's messaging carrier, after you see a preview and confirm. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Security

  • In transit: the app connects over TLS. Every request after sign-in carries your session token and is checked against your organisation, so one organisation's records are never served to another.
  • At rest: accounts, sessions, connection credentials and FACE's application tables are encrypted (AES-GCM envelope encryption). Two exceptions: the spoken-reply cache is not encrypted, and storage file names are not encrypted.

How long we keep it

  • Sessions: 12 hours, or until you sign out.
  • Live camera frames and voice audio: not kept.
  • Grading results, questions and attachment text: no automatic expiry today.
  • Evidence images: not kept unless the operator turns them on; then shown for at most 30 days.
  • Your account record: until you delete it in the app, or your administrator removes it.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings › Account › Delete my account. The dialog says what is deleted and what your organisation keeps; type the email address you sign in with, then choose Delete my account permanently. The server deletes your account record, ends every session of yours and deletes the follow-ups, scenario-builder entries and unfinished mailbox connections you own; only then does the app sign you out and clear this device. If you are your organisation's only admin it refuses, and if the server cannot be reached your account is not deleted. Records your organisation keeps show a fixed code (former-member-…) instead of you. The account deletion page describes the same path and, if you can no longer open the app, the request by email to contact@runink.org with the subject "Delete my FACE account", sent from the address you sign in with.

Your organisation's administrator can also remove you, which deletes your account record and ends your sessions. Data that belongs to your organisation (its runs, decisions and grading results) is deleted at your organisation's request.

To ask what is stored about you, or to have it corrected or exported, email contact@runink.org.

Children

FACE is a business console. It is not directed at children.

This website

This page is on docs.runink.org. That site uses Google Analytics only if you accept its cookie banner. The app does not.

Changes

When FACE's behaviour changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
