LUNA

This is the documentation for Runink LUNA (Lifestyle Understanding & Navigation Assistant), a personal companion app for habits, training and meals. LUNA is in development and not yet available in Google Play or the App Store; access is for approved accounts only. These pages describe what is built today and say plainly what is not.

Start where you are:

  • Using LUNA: the User guide covers every feature, and Concepts explains how one Luna, its six aspects and the plans fit together.
  • Running LUNA for approved people: the Admin guide covers accounts, plan state, removing access and deletion requests.
  • Building on LUNA: the Developer guide covers the API, the services and the data model, and Architecture shows how the parts connect.

The overview has the full status of each area. Prices and plans are on the product page at runink.org, not in this documentation.
