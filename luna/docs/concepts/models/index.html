Model cards

Model cards

A model card says, in plain words, what one part of Luna's AI does, what it reads, what it produces, where it runs, and the limits it follows. There is one card per aspect, one for drafting plans, one for the on-device model, and one for safety.

The cards

  • Trainer: Training, workouts, form and recovery, from what you logged.
  • Nutritionist: Meals, macros, hydration and plan adherence, around your targets.
  • Keeper: Day planning around fixed appointments. Advises; never books.
  • Counsel: Decisions and brainstorming: the trade-off, a recommendation, what would change it.
  • Muse: Creative directions, naming and honest critique. Original work only.
  • Naturalist: Animals: species, behaviour and care. Vet first when it matters.
  • On-device model: The small model on your phone: the free tier and offline answers.
  • Plan drafting: Revisions to training plans and the foods in a meal plan. Nothing is live until you save.
  • Safety: What Luna refuses and who it points you to instead.

What every card has in common

One Luna. The six aspects are not six separate models: one model answers, with the aspect that fits in front (see Six aspects, one Luna). Each card describes how Luna behaves when that aspect is in front.

The model. Luna's server text work (paid plan, coming) runs on an open model.

Where it runs. On the free tier, chat runs on the on-device model on your phone. On the paid plan (coming), chat, plans, photo features and speech-to-text run on Runink-operated servers. No third-party AI service is ever called. The apps do still contact a few outside services, such as Google Sign-In and, on the phone apps, the phone's own speech engine, which reads Luna's replies aloud. The full list is on Privacy and data.

Real numbers. Streaks, points, levels, nutrition targets and weekly set counts come from what you logged.
