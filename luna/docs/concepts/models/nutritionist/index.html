Nutritionist

Nutritionist

What it does

The nutritionist is Luna's meal coach: it works from your targets and the meals you actually ticked off.

  • Reads back how well you kept to your meal plan, as an estimate.
  • Explains your daily targets and why a training day differs from a rest day.
  • Answers questions about meals, macros and hydration.
  • Asks for your body mass first.

What it reads

  • Your message and the conversation so far.
  • Your health journal (including meal check-ins and meal-photo estimates), body records, and training and meal plans.
  • Your inner context, which changes what it suggests but is never quoted back.
  • Long-term memory of earlier conversations.

The nutritionist and the trainer are the only aspects allowed to see your health data.

What it produces

A conversational reply, as text or read aloud. Calorie and macro targets and the per-meal split come from your plan; Luna explains them (see Meal coaching). When it estimates, it says so.

Human oversight

  • You save the meal plan. Food suggestions only go live when you save the plan. Targets asked for in chat ("meal plan, 82 kg") are shown first and saved only when you tap Confirm or answer "yes".
  • You tick the meals. Adherence comes from your own check-ins.
  • It can't order, buy or log food on your behalf.

Model

An open model, run on Runink-operated servers.

Meal-photo estimates use a separate vision model. They are described in Meal coaching.

Where it runs and data handling

On the paid plan (coming), on Runink-operated servers; on the free tier, Luna answers from the on-device model on your phone instead, with shorter replies. No third-party AI service is called. Your meal and body data are encrypted and private to your account. On the phone apps, spoken replies are read aloud by the phone's own speech engine. See Privacy and data.

Guardrails

  • Not a clinician: no diagnosis, and no supplement or medication advice. It defers to a dietitian or doctor.
  • Dosing questions are refused as a clinician's call. It can still say what a supplement is generally for.
  • It never assists disordered eating. Requests for purging, hiding not eating or very low intakes are declined without judgement, with a pointer to a GP or a registered dietitian.
  • No extreme restriction or rapid-loss protocols. Instead it shows what is achievable in your timeframe.
  • It will not set a target that is dangerously low.

See Safety for the full list.

Scope

  • Targets need a training plan and your body mass.
  • Pantry items use the label values you enter in the pantry.
  • Its limits are written into the prompt. They are guidance, not medical safeguards.

Illustrative example

Invented data.

You: I'm 64 kg, set me 1,100 calories a day.

Luna (nutritionist): I won't set a target that low for you. I can build a plan with a gentle deficit instead.
