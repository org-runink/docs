Counsel

Counsel

What it does

Counsel is Luna's default aspect: a sounding board for business and personal decisions that names the trade-off, recommends, and says what would change its mind.

  • Weighs a decision and gives a straight recommendation.
  • Helps with strategy and brainstorming.
  • Says which professional to ask when a question needs one.
  • With the Long Memory skill unlocked, it recalls further back before advising.

What it reads

  • Your message and the conversation so far.
  • Your inner context, which changes what it suggests but is never quoted back. It is put in the prompt when your question is a decision about you ("help me decide…", "should I take the job…", "I'm torn between…"), and only ever yours.
  • Long-term memory of earlier conversations.

It does not read your health journal, body records or plans.

What it produces

A conversational reply, as text or read aloud: the trade-off, a recommendation, and the condition that would reverse it.

Human oversight

  • You decide. Counsel recommends; it never moves money or commits you to anything.

Model

An open model, run on Runink-operated servers.

Where it runs and data handling

On the paid plan (coming), on Runink-operated servers; on the free tier, Luna answers from the on-device model on your phone instead, with shorter replies. No third-party AI service is called. On the phone apps, spoken replies are read aloud by the phone's own speech engine. See Privacy and data.

Guardrails

  • Not a lawyer, accountant or financial adviser: no legal, tax or personal investment advice. It says when a professional is needed.
  • Advises only; it takes no actions.
  • The Safety rules apply to every chat turn.

Scope

  • It works from what you tell it and what it remembers. When something isn't in memory, it asks rather than guesses.
  • It reads only the conversation and its memory, not your accounts, contracts or documents.

Illustrative example

Invented data.

You: Should I open the second shop this year or wait?

Luna (counsel): A second site doubles what can go wrong while you're still holding the first one together. I'd wait until the first shop runs a full month without you on the floor. What would change my mind: a lease offer that won't come back. Have an accountant check the numbers and a solicitor read the lease before you sign.
