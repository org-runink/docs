Keeper

Keeper

What it does

The keeper helps you shape a day around the things that can't move, keeps an eye on your streaks, and says plainly that it hasn't booked anything.

  • Fits flexible tasks around fixed appointments, with some slack.
  • Tells you how your training, meal-plan and step streaks stand, and which one is at risk because today isn't logged yet.
  • Keeps in mind what you've told it before, such as a regular late shift.
  • With the Day Shape skill unlocked, it gives a concrete day plan rather than a list (see Levels, traits and skills).

What it reads

  • Your message and the conversation so far.
  • Long-term memory of earlier conversations.
  • Your streak counts, when you ask about your streaks or what not to skip: how many days in a row for each habit, and whether today is logged yet. These come from what you logged.

It does not read the values in your health journal (steps, sessions, meals), your body records, plans or inner context.

What it produces

A short, timed plan or a streak check-in, in a conversational reply, as text or read aloud. A day plan is for you to copy into your own calendar.

Human oversight

  • You decide and you book. Everything it proposes is advice.
  • No calendar, mail or messaging is connected. It never claims to have read, created, moved or cancelled anything. The only reminders are the optional ones you set up yourself under Profile, Gentle reminders; it cannot create, change or cancel them.

Model

An open model, run on Runink-operated servers.

Where it runs and data handling

On the paid plan (coming), on Runink-operated servers; on the free tier, Luna answers from the on-device model on your phone instead, with shorter replies. No third-party AI service is called. On the phone apps, spoken replies are read aloud by the phone's own speech engine. See Privacy and data.

Guardrails

  • Advises only; it cannot act.
  • Reasons only from what you say, what it remembers and your streak counts. With no streak data, it says so rather than guess.
  • The Safety rules apply to every chat turn.

Illustrative example

Invented data.

You: Tomorrow I've got the dentist at 10:30 and a team meeting at 14:00. Where do I fit groceries and two hours on the report?

Luna (keeper): 08:00–10:00 the report. 10:30 dentist. 11:30–12:15 groceries. 14:00 team meeting. Nothing here is booked: I can't touch your calendar and I can't set a reminder for you. You can switch on gentle reminders yourself under Profile.
