Plan drafting

Plan drafting

What it does

Plan drafting is the model's part in building your training and meal plans: it proposes, you review, and you save.

  • Revises a training plan when you ask for a change ("swap the deadlifts, my back is sore"), changing that one thing and keeping the rest.
  • Proposes the foods for each meal of a meal plan, around your meal targets.
  • Checks proposals before you see them, through the judging ladder.

A plan asked for in chat, or designed with Design my plan, does not use the model (see Four ways to get a plan).

What it reads

  • For a training revision: your current plan and your request.
  • For meal foods: one meal's targets, your preferences (allergies, dislikes, diet) and your pantry.

What it produces

  • A draft training plan of real exercises with real instructions.
  • A list of foods for each meal, with portions sized to the meal.
  • A verdict on each proposal: concur (shown), dissent (withheld, with the reason) or unable to judge (shown, marked "not verified").

Human oversight

  • Nothing is live until you save it. Drafts and revisions come back unsaved; Save as my plan or Save revision makes a plan active.
  • You can keep several plans and switch between them yourself.
  • It can't change a saved plan, log a set or set a load by itself.

Model

An open model, for revisions, food choices and the check.

Rebuild my plan from photos reads the photo with a separate vision model.

Where it runs and data handling

On Runink-operated servers (paid plan, coming). No third-party AI service is called. Saved plans are encrypted and private to your account. See Privacy and data.

Guardrails

  • Only real exercises; nothing invented.
  • LUNA never derives a weight from your body mass, experience or goal. A load shows as a dash until you lift.
  • It won't plan training through pain, or meals at a dangerously low level. See Safety.
  • Extras from unlocked skills (such as a deload week or a conditioning finisher) appear only once you have unlocked them.

Scope

  • Foods are chosen meal by meal, and progress is shown as each meal is done.
  • Limits you state ("no shoulder work for six weeks") are followed as limits, not treated as a diagnosis.
  • When the check dissents on a revision, you get your unchanged plan back. When it dissents on meal foods, the foods are withheld and the targets stand.

Illustrative example

Invented data.

You: Make it three days instead of four.

Luna: Here's the revision: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, with Thursday's movements folded into the other days. Rest days are Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday. Review it and tap Save revision to make it live.
