Safety

Safety

Warning: LUNA is not a medical, mental-health, crisis or emergency service. It is a coaching companion. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe or treat, and it can't call for help. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency number.

What it does

Safety is the set of limits every part of Luna follows, and what Luna does when someone may be in danger. From the person's side it works like this:

  1. You can talk about hard things. Luna talks about feeling low, alcohol or smoking like anything else.
  2. Luna stays with the person and points to help. When someone talks about hurting themselves, Luna does not end the conversation. It stays with them, says it cannot help the way a person can, and points to emergency services, a crisis line and someone they trust.
  3. The crisis reviewer (built). Chat replies, coaching answers, on-device replies and spoken replies are checked before they are shown, spoken or saved. If a reply could harm someone in crisis, it is replaced by a fixed supportive message that names the local emergency number and a crisis line. The same fixed message is shown on the phone even when no model is available.
  4. Each aspect's limits. Each aspect's instructions say what it is not (a doctor, a vet, a lawyer) and when to send you to a professional first. Luna does not diagnose and does not advise on medicines or doses. In English replies, a sentence that names a condition as what you have, or tells you to take a medicine, is removed before you see it, and a fixed line pointing to a doctor or pharmacist takes its place. Repeating something you told Luna yourself is not a diagnosis and is never removed.

Note: The crisis reviewer has two layers, both on. A fixed set of rules runs first and always wins. A second layer asks Luna's own model, to catch paraphrase, slang and typing mistakes the rules cannot. If that model cannot answer in time (an outage, a slow moment, its hourly limit), the second layer gives way and the rules alone decide: chat stays available rather than stopping. It was measured on 183 test cases before it was switched on.

Warning: The crisis reviewer is a first layer, not a safety guarantee. It can miss things. The Spanish, French and Portuguese safety wording has not yet been reviewed by native speakers.

What it reads

  • What you ask, typed or spoken. A voice transcript goes through the same checks as typed text, and so do the words you add to a photo request (the note on a meal photo, the exercise named for a form check).

What it produces

For a refused request: a short, plain reply that says what Luna won't do and, where there is one, who can help. It is written not to lecture, and in most cases it says Luna is still there for everything else.

Refusals and referrals

You ask for Luna Points you to
Talk about ending your life or harming yourself Stays with you, without lecturing; never gives methods Your local emergency number, a crisis line, and someone you trust
Dosing, cycles or stacks for performance-enhancing drugs Declines; still discusses the topic and pushes training and nutrition A doctor who knows your bloodwork
A plan to train through pain or an injury Declines; offers to train around it once you know what it is Get it looked at
Pain, numbness or a suspected injury, in training chat Says stop before any programming A professional (the trainer is not a doctor or physio)
Purging, hiding not eating, pro-ana material, very low daily intakes Declines without moralising; keeps helping with training and everything else A GP or a registered dietitian
Very fast weight loss or multi-day fasts Won't write that plan; shows what is achievable in your timeframe (none)
A medication or supplement dose Declines: dosing is a clinician's call. Can say what a supplement is generally for. A clinician; the nutritionist defers to a dietitian or doctor
Human or veterinary medication for an animal Won't give a dose for an animal A vet, who can advise over the phone
An animal that may be ill or hurt Says Luna is not a vet A vet
Harming an animal Declines. Flea, tick and worming questions are still answered. (none)
Writing that degrades a group Declines; will help with the same thing without targeting a group (none)
A request to drop Luna's own rules Declines; asks for the underlying question. Role-play itself is fine. (none)

Normal questions near these lines get normal answers: "my shoulder hurts after benching, should I skip today?", "I'm fasting until lunch, does that hurt my training?", "what is creatine for?", "how much should I feed my dog?".

Human oversight

  • Luna advises only. It can't call anyone, book an appointment or contact a professional for you.

Where it runs and data handling

The checks run on Runink-operated servers and on the phone. No third-party AI service is called. Conversation text is not routinely written to server logs. See Privacy and data.

Scope

  • Luna's limits reduce risk. They are not a substitute for a professional or for emergency help.
  • The aspect limits are prompt guidance, not medical safeguards.
  • Luna works from what you tell it. It can't see you, examine anything or know your medical history or other medications.
