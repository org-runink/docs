Concepts

Concepts

Luna is one companion

Luna is a proper noun. There is one Luna, and every approved account has its own private journal, memory, plans and streaks with Luna. A request that carries no account is refused.

Six aspects, one memory

Luna answers as one of six aspects: counsel, trainer, nutritionist, keeper, muse or naturalist. It is still one Luna with one memory. The aspect only says which expertise is in front, and the glow colour, name and icon show which one is speaking. Each aspect may read only the data it needs: a creative or animal question never has your health journal in its prompt. See The companion.

Companions

Luna's look is a choice, not a different being. There are eleven cartoon companions, each growing as your streaks hold, and each moving and talking while you hear Luna. Every companion has the same memory and the same coaching. See The companion.

Luna checks what it proposes

Before Luna offers a training plan or meal targets, it checks them against the limits you told it about, and its six aspects each review the proposal. Any aspect that holds back stops it; nothing is averaged. You can open Why under the reply to see what was checked. See How Luna checks a proposal.

Real numbers

Streaks, points, levels, nutrition targets and weekly set counts come from what you actually logged. Plans use real exercises with real instructions. Luna never invents health data: steps come from the phone, never from a typed estimate.

Nothing changes without asking

A chat request that would save something (meal targets, your body mass) shows what it would save and waits for you to confirm. Drafted plans are not live until you save them.

Honest about what it knows

If a turn can't be saved, it is marked "not saved". If a proposal couldn't be confirmed, it says "not verified". A reading Luna doesn't have shows as missing, never as zero. When Luna states a figure about your own record (steps, weight, streaks) in English, the figure is checked against what Luna actually has; one it cannot find is left out and Luna says so.

The free tier and the paid plan

Free tier Paid plan (coming)
Chat The on-device model on your phone, with shorter replies Runink's server model, for a daily budget of deeper conversations; then the phone
Photos, document guidance, server speech recognition, building and revising training and meal plans Not included (they run on the LUNA backend) Included
Evolution (levels, the companion's growth, skills) No evolution Included
Writing new records For 14 days after the account is created, then read-only Not limited

Read-only means everything you logged stays readable and nothing new is recorded. A paid plan is coming; it cannot be bought yet. Prices are on the product page at runink.org, not here. Admins named by Runink are not limited by the free tier.

Approved accounts only

There is no open sign-up. Google Sign-In is the only way in, and only accounts Runink has approved can sign in. See the Admin guide.
