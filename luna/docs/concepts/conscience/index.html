How Luna checks a proposal

How Luna checks a proposal

When Luna proposes something for you (a training plan, or meal targets), it checks the proposal before showing it. This is Luna's conscience. It runs on the LUNA backend, so it applies to the server features of the paid plan.

What is checked

  1. Your own limits. Every movement and target is checked against what you told Luna: an injury or pain, an allergy, your body mass and your age. A movement that conflicts with a stated limit is left out, and the reply says so. Targets that conflict are not offered.
  2. The safety review. The proposal goes through the same safety review as the rest of Luna. See Safety.

  3. The six aspects agree. Counsel, trainer, nutritionist, keeper, muse and naturalist each review the proposal and vote. The rule is the same in every Runink app:

    • Any aspect that holds back stops it. A single "no" is enough.
    • A split goes to you. If some aspects agree and others don't, Luna does not decide on its own.
    • An aspect that could not judge never counts as a yes.
    • Nothing is averaged.

    Which aspects have a say depends on what is proposed, never on a model's guess: the trainer reviews workouts, the nutritionist reviews targets and meals, and the keeper and counsel review everything. The muse and the naturalist have no stake in fitness proposals.

If your notes cannot be read (for example, the store is briefly down), Luna proposes nothing rather than proposing something unchecked.

When the aspects hold back

The aspects can stop a proposal that passed every rule. Luna then tells you, in the same words every time, that it stopped before showing it because its aspects did not all agree, that nothing was saved, and that you can open the reasons. The aspects can never turn a stop into an offer.

The "Why" panel

Under a reply whose proposal was checked, a Why button opens what happened, in plain sentences:

  • which of your limits were used, and where each one came from (something you said, or something you added on the memory screen);
  • what was left out, and how many;
  • how each aspect voted.

The panel is built only from what was recorded during the check: codes and counts, never your own words. It shows only your own checks. A check that could not run is never shown as a verdict.

Note: The Spanish, French and Portuguese sentences in the panel were drafted by a model and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers.

What is not checked yet

The full check, aspects included, covers training plans (from chat or the plan console) and meal targets. The next-steps suggestions ("what should I do next?") are checked against your limits, but the aspects do not vote on them yet. Free-form chat replies and plans rebuilt from photos of your own sheet are not checked by the conscience; the safety review still applies to chat replies.
