Getting Started

Getting Started

Note: LUNA is not yet available in any store. This page describes the app as it is built today, for approved test accounts.

Before you start

  • Your Google account must be approved. LUNA has no sign-up form. Runink approves accounts (see the Admin guide).
  • Google Sign-In is the only way in. There is no password.

1. Sign in

The first screen shows Luna and one button, Sign in with Google. Pick the approved account. The server checks Google's token, checks that the address is verified and approved, and opens a session that lasts 12 hours.

If sign-in fails, the message tells you why:

Message What it means
this account is not on this luna instance's allowlist The Google account is valid, but it isn't approved. Ask Runink to add it.
google email not verified Google reports the address as unverified.
invalid google token The token from Google failed verification. Try signing in again.
cannot reach Google to verify this sign-in — … The server couldn't reach Google. The problem is on the server side, not with your account.

2. Choose Luna's companion

On first sign-in the app opens the Choose Luna carousel. Swipe through the eleven cartoon companions (Aussie, Husky, Fox, Cat, Owl, Ferret, Bear, Monkey, Crow, Hummingbird, Mockingbird), type a name in Name Luna, and tap Bind companion to choose it.

  • The name can't be empty ("Luna needs a name.").
  • or upload a custom video loop uses a video you pick as Luna's idle loop instead of a drawn companion.

Every companion is the same Luna, with the same memory and the same coaching. You can change it later (see The companion).

3. The one screen

LUNA has no tabs. The whole app is Luna on the meadow backdrop, with the daily cards above. There is no chat button: double-tap Luna to talk.

  • Luna, animated, reacting to listening, thinking and speaking, and to touch.
  • The daily cards: Training, Meals and Steps, where you check in (see Habits and streaks).
  • The level ring: Luna's level and progress toward the next one (see Levels, traits and skills).
  • The chat card, opened by double-tapping Luna (double-tap again, tap outside, swipe the header down or press X to close). It has the composer ("Say anything…"), the mic, a camera button, an attach button (Show Luna something), the speaker toggle, the six aspects (Auto by default), and your stats, collapsed until you tap them.

The small ⋯ menu at the top left leads to everything else:

Menu item What it opens
Profile & connections Your profile, data connections (steps, body, sleep, weather), gentle reminders, pets, how Luna sounds, and the guide to the aspects
Training plan The plan console: Overview, Training and Meals views of the week, and the pantry
Session history Past conversations
Track an activity A GPS-tracked walk, run or ride
Wear a different form The companion switcher: who Luna is and how it grows
Open-source licences Third-party notices
Sign out Ends the session on the server

4. First things to try

  1. Ask for a training plan. Type something like "make me a 4 day workout plan". Review the draft deck and save it. The Training card then follows that plan.
  2. Ask for meal targets. Type "meal plan, 82 kg" (with your own body mass). Meal targets come from the training week, so make the training plan first. Luna saves them only when you tap Confirm or answer "yes".
  3. Check in. Tap Session done on the Training card after a workout and tick off meals as you eat them. On Android, connect steps through Health Connect on the profile page.
  4. Just talk. Luna picks the aspect that fits, and the colour of the glow tells you which one is answering.

During the first week, a daily tip introduces one aspect each day. On the free tier some features need the paid plan, which is coming; see Free tier and paid plan.
