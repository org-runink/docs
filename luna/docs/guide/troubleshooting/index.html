Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting

LUNA says what went wrong in plain words rather than showing a raw error.

Signing in

Message What to do
this account is not on this luna instance's allowlist The account isn't approved. Ask Runink to add it.
google email not verified / invalid google token Sign in again with Google.
cannot reach Google to verify this sign-in — … A server-side problem, not your account. Try again later.
You are returned to the sign-in screen Your 12-hour session ended, or your access was removed. Sign in again.

Chat

Message Meaning
Luna is unreachable — check your connection and try again. The request never got an answer. On the phone, the on-device model takes over while you are offline (where it is installed).
the model plane is busy right now — … Every slot on the shared inference server is in use. Try again in a moment.
the model did not answer in time. … The request took longer than Luna waits. Try again.
We've talked a lot this hour and the thinking plane is shared — give me about N minutes and ask again. … The shared backend is protecting itself from overload. This is not a usage allowance: logging habits and your plans still work instantly.
The free plan chats with the model on this phone, and it cannot answer right now. The on-device model is not installed or cannot run on this device.
Free accounts chat with the model on this device only, … A server feature was asked for on the free tier. See Free tier and paid plan.
the free period ended on <day> (14 days after the account was created), so I am read-only now: … The free account is read-only. Everything you logged is still readable.
A reply marked "not saved" The reply was delivered but couldn't be written to your journal, so it won't be remembered.

Photos and voice

Message Meaning
my eyes aren't wired to a vision model yet — … Photo features are not available on this service right now.
vision is rate-limited on the shared plane — try again in ~N min The shared photo budget for this hour is used.
N photos is more than I can look at in one go — send up to 3 at a time Send at most 3 images at a time.
voice is rate-limited on the shared plane — try again in ~N min The shared voice budget for this hour is used. Text chat still works.
Luna says it is using the phone's recogniser The server recogniser was unavailable for that turn. Say it again.

Coaching

Message Meaning
design a training plan first — meal targets are derived from the training week, so the two agree. Make a training plan before meal targets.
I need your body mass in kg first — … Add your body mass in your profile or in the request.
level N costs C points — you have B. Keep the streaks alive. Not enough points for that level yet.
Levels, forms and steps on better hardware are not part of the free plan. Evolution is part of the paid plan, which is coming.
a daily steps goal needs to be between 1,000 and 100,000 Pick a goal in that range.

For step-sync messages, see Habits and streaks.
