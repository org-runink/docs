Free tier and paid plan

Free tier and paid plan

Every approved account starts on the free tier. A paid plan is coming; it cannot be bought yet. Prices will be on the product page at runink.org, not in this documentation.

The free tier

  • Chat with the on-device model. Replies come from a small model on your phone, and are shorter. It does not answer health questions. See the on-device model card.
  • No conversation cap. Talk as much as you like.
  • No evolution. Levels, the companion's growth, the evolution stage and the persona skills stay at their first step. Your streaks, points and traits are still recorded.
  • No server model. Photo features, document guidance, server speech recognition and building or revising training and meal plans run on the LUNA backend, so they are not part of the free tier.
  • Read-only after 14 days. Fourteen days after your account was created, the account becomes read-only: everything you logged stays readable, and nothing new is recorded. Luna tells you the day it ended.

What stays available on the free tier: the daily cards, streaks, the habit score and vitality, Health Connect steps, the pantry, the barcode scanner, pets, reminders, weather, and everything you already saved, within the 14-day rule above.

The paid plan (coming)

  • Chat with Runink's server model, within a daily budget of deeper conversations (below); after that Luna answers from the phone.
  • Evolution: levels, the evolution stage, the cartoon companions' growth and the persona skills.
  • Every server feature: photos, documents, server speech recognition, plan building and revision.
  • No 14-day read-only limit.

Your journal is never reset. When an account moves to the paid plan, Luna picks up from what is already recorded.

Deeper conversations each day

On the paid plan, chat with the server model has a daily budget of deeper conversations. Luna never stops talking to you because of it:

  • Three deeper conversations a day, each up to four replies from the server model.
  • Walking adds more. Every 2,000 steps your phone measures adds one more, up to two extra a day. Steps you type in never count.
  • Nothing carries over, and the day is your own local day.
  • Only replies the server model writes count. Saving plans, confirming, answering yes or no, and the next-steps suggestions are free.

When one deeper conversation is left, a calm line near the composer says so. When they are used, it says that Luna keeps answering on this phone, and replies come from the on-device model until the next day. As on the free tier, the on-device model does not answer health questions. There is no always-on counter and nothing rewards chatting for its own sake.

If the count cannot be read, the reply comes from the phone rather than being spent on the server. The free tier is never metered (it does not use the server model).

Admins

Admins named by Runink are never limited by the free tier. See the Admin guide.
