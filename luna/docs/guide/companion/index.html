The companion

The companion

Luna is a proper noun. There is one Luna, and it appears as one of eleven cartoon companions. The look is only an image you feel close to: every companion has the same memory, the same coaching and the same history.

The eleven companions

Aussie (an Australian Shepherd, the default), Husky, Fox, Cat, Owl, Ferret, Bear, Monkey, Crow, Hummingbird and Mockingbird.

Every companion is a hand-made illustration in the same cartoon sticker style, with a thick dark outline. The Aussie has three pictures (calm at its laptop, singing, and happily digging). The other ten are each drawn from one picture that Luna's expressions are made from: at rest its eyes are open, its mouth or beak is closed and it looks at you; only when it talks does its mouth open with the words and the little sound marks appear. When it is glad or asleep its eyes close in the drawn smile.

On first sign-in you choose a companion and give Luna a name (see Getting Started). After that, ⋯ → Wear a different form opens the switcher, where you can pick another companion and the way it grows. Your choice is kept on the phone. Nothing is re-bound and no history changes.

A custom avatar is a set of uploaded video loops, one per state (idle, listening, thinking, speaking). An idle loop is required, and any missing state falls back to idle.

Luna moves and talks

The companion is never a still picture. Its whole body breathes and leans slowly, its head drifts and looks about with the ears and tail following a moment later, and it blinks at uneven intervals, the way an animal does. Every motion eases in and out, so nothing snaps or jitters. Every few seconds it does something: it cocks its head, flicks an ear, looks around, perks up or taps its paws. While it is thinking, the Aussie types on its laptop. Moving from one pose to the next (idle, listening, thinking, talking, happy, sleeping) blends smoothly instead of jumping.

  • It talks while you hear it. When a reply is spoken, Luna's mouth moves for exactly as long as the sentence plays, and follows the words. Where the phone reports each word as it is spoken, the mouth follows those words; where it doesn't, it moves at a natural, uneven pace, never like a metronome.
  • While a reply is being written with the voice off, Luna mouths along.
  • While the mic is open, Luna takes its listening pose and waits.
  • Reduce motion. If your phone asks apps to reduce or remove animations, Luna stops swaying and gesturing, but it still blinks, breathes and moves its mouth when it talks.

How Luna grows

In the switcher you choose which way your companion grows. The illustrations for each growth stage are still to be drawn, so for now the choice is kept and the picture stays the same:

  • Bright: a cheerful companion that collects things to wear as it levels up.
  • Beast: the body itself changes, stage by stage, like a role-playing game: Pup, Hound, Warhound, Beast, Alpha.

Level 1 is always the plain, simplest look, and each level keeps everything the level before it had. Levels never go down. The Steps card tells you what is still missing for the next level, in your own units. See Levels, traits and skills.

Note: Evolution is part of the paid plan, which is coming. On the free tier Luna does not evolve: the level and the skills stay at their first step. Your streaks, points and traits are still recorded, so nothing is lost.

Six aspects, one Luna

Every reply comes from one of six aspects. You never pick one; you just ask, and Luna answers with the aspect that fits. The companion is tinted by the aspect's colour, and each reply also carries the aspect's name and icon, so colour is never the only cue.

Aspect Domain Its limits
Counsel (default) Business and personal decisions, strategy, brainstorming Not a lawyer, accountant or financial adviser. Never moves money or commits you to anything.
Trainer Training, workouts, form, recovery Not a doctor or physio. For pain, numbness or a suspected injury, it says to stop and see a professional first.
Nutritionist Meals, macros, hydration, plan adherence Not a clinician. No diagnosis, supplements, medication, extreme restriction or rapid-loss protocols. Never assists disordered eating.
Keeper Agenda, scheduling, day planning, your streaks No calendar, mail or messaging is connected. It can advise, but it never claims to have read, created or moved anything. The only reminders are the optional ones you switch on yourself under Profile, Gentle reminders; it cannot create, change or cancel them.
Muse Creative work, writing, design, music, naming Original work only: no song lyrics or substantial copyrighted passages.
Naturalist Animals: species, behaviour, care, identification Not a vet. It does not replace one.

These limits are advisory: they shape the reply. What is enforced is data minimisation: each aspect may read only some of your data.

Aspect Health journal, body and plans Streak counts only Your "inner context" Memory
Trainer, Nutritionist yes (included in the journal) yes yes
Counsel no no yes yes
Keeper no yes no yes
Muse, Naturalist no no no yes

So a creative or animal question never has your health journal in its prompt. Personal data goes in only when the question is about you and touches that data: a training or food question for the journal, plans and inner context, a decision question for your inner context (counsel), a streak or "what not to skip" question for the streak counts. It is always marked to the model as data, not instructions.

Nothing in chat changes your data without asking. When a chat request would save something (meal targets, your body mass), Luna shows what it would save and waits: tap Confirm or answer "yes" as your next message. Anything else, or 10 minutes, drops it unsaved.

"How I got here": reasoning boxes

While Luna works on something, progress appears as small comic dialogue boxes stacked beside the companion. These are not chat turns and are never saved. Each box reports only what actually happened, never an invented step. When the shared model is at capacity, Luna says so instead of inventing a queue position.

The meadow backdrop

Luna's screens sit on a hand-drawn dusk meadow in the same cartoon style as the characters: flat colours, a thick dark outline, rolling hills, trees, flowers and a few slow clouds. It is the same on every screen and does not change with the clock.

Local weather is an opt-in connection on the profile page, on the phone app and the test build alike. When it is on, the scene mirrors the real sky (rain, snow, fog or storm). The weather is fetched by the app itself from Open-Meteo, with your location coarsened to about 1 km, and refreshed every 30 minutes. It never goes through the LUNA backend. The location permission is asked only when you tap Connect.
