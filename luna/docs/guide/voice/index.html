Voice

Voice

Note: Voice is built and tested in code, but it has not yet been heard on a released device, because nothing is released yet.

Every spoken conversation opens with the AI statement

Before the first reply Luna speaks, and again after a long silence, Luna says: "This is an automated AI assistant from Runink." The wording is shared by every Runink voice agent and the app cannot change it. It is spoken in the app's language (English, Spanish, French or Portuguese).

Two ways to talk

  • Dictate a message. Tap the mic in the chat card and speak. The turn ends after a short silence, and the words are sent as an ordinary chat message.
  • Talk with Luna, hands-free. Press and hold the mic. Luna listens, answers aloud, and listens again. You can interrupt by speaking or by tapping ("Luna is speaking. Speak, or tap here, to interrupt."); a short "mm" or "yeah" does not interrupt. Headphones work best: on the loudspeaker, Luna can hear itself. A device that cannot listen while it speaks makes you take turns, and says so.

How your words are recognised

The app records the audio and sends it to Runink's own speech recogniser, which runs on Runink's servers, not a third party's. Server speech recognition uses the server model, so on the free tier it is not used, and it has not yet been run in live use.

When the server recogniser is not available, the app uses the phone's own recogniser and says so, asking you to repeat what you said (the phone cannot hear what was said before it started). On most Android phones the phone's recogniser is Google's. An expired session never falls back: the app asks you to sign in again.

Hearing Luna

Replies are spoken by the phone's own voice (its text-to-speech engine), on the phone. The speaker toggle in the chat card turns spoken replies on or off, and the setting is remembered. Markdown is not read aloud. Each aspect gets its own pitch and pace, and its own voice where the phone offers several in your language; where it offers one, every aspect sounds alike apart from a slight change of pitch and pace.

While a reply is spoken, the companion's mouth moves with it, sentence by sentence and, where the phone reports them, word by word. See Luna moves and talks.

Spoken replies are checked by the crisis reviewer too. If it stops a reply, Luna stops speaking and the text chat shows a fixed supportive message instead. See Safety.

Warning: The phone's engines are third parties. The phone's text-to-speech engine receives the text of Luna's replies, and the phone's recogniser (when used) receives your voice. No model provider receives your voice through the LUNA backend.

Limits

  • Voice requests share an hourly budget that protects the shared backend. Over it, Luna says voice is rate-limited and how many minutes to wait; text chat keeps working.
  • One recording can be up to 16 MiB of audio.
  • If nothing was said, Luna replies "I didn't catch any words in that — try again a little closer."
