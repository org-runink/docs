Levels, traits and skills

Levels, traits and skills

Luna grows as your streaks hold. Your streaks earn points, points buy levels, and the walking you do earns traits that unlock persona skills.

Points and levels

Training, meals and steps each keep a streak, and keeping the streaks alive earns points. Points buy Luna levels, from level 0 up to a maximum of 10.

Levels never go down. A broken streak can leave your balance at zero, but a bought level stays bought. Luna never gets worse because of a bad week. A lapse softens Luna a little, and there is no shaming.

The level ring on the home screen shows the level, and the arc shows progress to the next one. When you can afford a level, the ring turns gold and reads "Level N is ready — tap to level up! (X pts)". Tapping it calls HabitService.LevelUp. At level 10 it reads "Luna is at the highest level".

If you tap without enough points, Luna answers FAILED_PRECONDITION and says how many points you have and how many the level costs. At the top it answers that Luna is already at the highest level.

A level changes how the companion looks (what it wears on the Bright path, its stage on the Beast path) and helps unlock skills (below).

On the free tier

On the free tier Luna does not evolve: your progress is recorded, but nothing advances, and Luna says so rather than going quiet. Tapping the ring answers "Levels, forms and steps on better hardware are not part of the free plan."

Your streaks, points and traits are recorded exactly as you earned them, so on the paid plan (which is coming) Luna picks up from what is already there.

Fourteen days after your account was created, a free account becomes read-only: everything you logged stays readable, and nothing new is recorded. See Free tier and paid plan.

Traits: what the walking earns

Traits are permanent milestones that grow with the steps you walk. They never go down.

Trait What it reflects
Wayfarer: "Shows up and walks the day out, day after day." Days you reached your step goal
Strider: "Ground covered — every step ever counted." Steps walked over time

Traits shape how Luna changes over time and unlock the skills below.

Skills: one path per aspect

Every aspect has a skill path. A skill unlocks as your traits and Luna's level grow, and some are labelled "(advisory)", which means they adjust how Luna advises.

Aspect Skill Effect
Trainer Periodization Training plans gain week-by-week progression and a deload week
Trainer Conditioning Training days end with a conditioning finisher
Nutritionist Meal Timing Daily targets split into per-meal portions
Nutritionist Fuel Cycling Training days get an extra fuelling meal; rest days stay lean
Counsel Long Memory Recalls further back before advising (advisory)
Keeper Day Shape Structures advice as a concrete day plan, not a list (advisory)
Muse Wilder Palette Offers stranger, more divergent directions first (advisory)
Naturalist Field Notes Adds behavioural context and comparisons between species (advisory)

A locked coaching skill produces exactly the plan you'd get without the feature, not a worse one. Skills appear on the profile page's persona cards. The Steps card lists the next few with what is still missing, in your own units.
