User guide

User guide

These pages describe every feature of LUNA as the app and backend implement it today. LUNA is not yet available in any store; where a feature is built but not switched on or not yet verified on a device, the page says so.

  • Getting started: Sign in, choose a form, the one screen.
  • The companion: Eleven cartoon companions, six aspects, how Luna changes.
  • Chat, photos and documents: Talking with Luna, memory, and everything Luna can look at.
  • Voice: Spoken conversation, the AI statement, the phone's own voice.
  • Habits and streaks: The three daily cards, kept days, Health Connect steps.
  • Levels, traits and skills: Points, permanent levels, walking traits, persona skills.
  • Training plans: Plans, revisions, set logging, form review.
  • Nutrition and pantry: Targets, the meal checklist, the pantry, the barcode scanner.
  • Body, activity and weather: Profile, scale, sleep, tracked activities, local weather.
  • Pets: Your pets' records, weights, vet visits and the vet sheet.
  • Reminders, tone and languages: Gentle reminders, how Luna sounds, app languages, the widget.
  • Free tier and paid plan: What the free tier includes and what the coming paid plan adds.
  • Privacy and data: Where data lives, who can read it, deletion on request.
  • Troubleshooting: Messages you may see and what they mean.
