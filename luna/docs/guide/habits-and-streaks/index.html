Habits and streaks

Habits and streaks

LUNA tracks three habits: training, meals and steps. You don't create or name them. Each one has a card on the home screen, its own streak, and a fixed rule for what counts as a kept day.

Note: There are no custom habit categories. Every habit record is one of the HabitKind values in the API: HABIT_KIND_WORKOUT, HABIT_KIND_MEAL_ADHERENCE, HABIT_KIND_STEPS and HABIT_KIND_STEPS_ADHOC.

The daily cards

Card How you check in What gets journaled
Training Shows today's session from your active plan (or "Rest day" or "No plan yet"). Tap Session done. A WORKOUT event with value 1
Meals Opens today's plan as a checklist ("Check off what you actually ate. N% checked — 80% keeps the streak."). A MEAL_ADHERENCE event carrying the fraction checked so far
Steps Counted by your phone. Sync now refreshes it, Change goal sets the goal, and Log an activity adds steps your phone didn't count. STEPS snapshots of the running total, plus STEPS_ADHOC for typed extra steps

Checking in is meant to be a single gesture. The Training and Meals cards work against the plan you built with Luna. If there is no plan yet, the card tells you to ask for one in chat first.

What counts as a kept day

Habit The day qualifies when…
Training A workout was logged (value above 0)
Meals Adherence was at least 0.8, meaning 80% of the day's plan was checked off
Steps The day's steps reached at least 80% of your daily goal

Logging "I ate badly" every day doesn't build a streak of keeping to a plan. Only a day that meets the rule counts.

Streak rules

  • A streak breaks on a missed day, not at midnight. A run ends today if today is logged. Otherwise it ends yesterday and is marked at risk: still alive, but waiting for today. It breaks only when a whole day passes without a qualifying entry.
  • Days are local days. The day boundary follows the time zone the service is configured with. Where none is set, days are counted in UTC, so an evening session in a time zone behind UTC can land on the next day.
  • Each habit reports current_days, longest_days, logged_today and at_risk (the Streak message in HabitService.GetScore).

Steps: counted, never typed

Steps come from the phone, never from a form field:

  • Android: read from Health Connect (today's and yesterday's local-day totals), which needs the READ_STEPS permission.
  • Web (test build only): read from Google Fit's REST API.

The app records the phone's count for today as it rises, and closes yesterday's total on the first sync of a new day. The same walk is never counted twice.

Steps your phone never counted (a treadmill run with the phone on a shelf, say) go through Log an activity. You type the number, and it is journaled as STEPS_ADHOC and added to the day's synced maximum. Luna never estimates steps from a duration. The same dialog has a Counts as a training day checkbox, which logs a separate workout event.

If a step read produces nothing, the app says why:

Message Cause
Luna has no way to count steps on this device. Unsupported platform
Health Connect isn't set up on this phone yet — install it and Luna can read the steps your phone already counts. Health Connect missing
Health Connect needs an update on this phone before Luna can read your steps. Health Connect out of date
Steps are not connected yet — tap Connect and Luna will start counting with you. Not connected
Luna doesn't have permission to read your steps yet. You can grant it whenever you like. Permission refused
Luna could not reach your step data just now — worth another try in a moment. Read failed
No steps recorded yet — your phone has not counted any today. Genuinely no data

The daily goal defaults to 10,000. SetStepsGoal accepts values from 1,000 to 100,000 ("a daily steps goal needs to be between 1,000 and 100,000"). Goal changes are journaled like any other record, and the latest one wins.

The habit score

Alongside the streaks, you get a rolling habit score from 0.0 to 1.0 that follows your recent training, meals and steps. The score answers "am I on track"; the streak answers "don't be the one who breaks it".

The score drives Luna's vitality: the creature looks leaner and quicker as it rises, and softens when it falls. The score and streaks also go into the chat prompt for the trainer and nutritionist aspects, so their coaching is grounded in what you actually logged. The keeper gets the streak counts alone (how many days in a row, and whether today is logged yet), with no score and no logged values, so it can answer "how are my streaks doing?".

Streaks also earn points toward levels. See Levels, traits and skills.

Home-screen widget (Android, optional)

On Android you can add a small Luna widget to your home screen. It is off until you add it. It shows two numbers the app has already fetched from the score it shows you (your longest current streak in days, and your level), and tapping it opens the app. Until the app has a score, it reads "Open Luna" with no number. A home screen can be seen by other people, so the widget never shows conversation text, meals, health data or names, and it makes no network calls. Signing out clears the two numbers.
