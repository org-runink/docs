Privacy and data

Privacy and data

This page describes what LUNA stores, where, and who can read it. The published privacy policy is at runink.org/luna-privacy.

What is stored

Everything Luna keeps about you is encrypted and private to your account, on Runink's own storage:

  • Chat turns (text and voice transcripts), habit events, avatars, training and meal plans, body records and logged sets.
  • Long-term memory of earlier conversations.
  • Your sessions, which last 12 hours.
  • Avatar video loops (custom avatars only).

Who can read it

  • Only you, through your session. Every request is checked against the session and the approved-account list. A request that has no account is refused, and nothing is ever shared between accounts.
  • Only the aspects that need it. Before the model runs, the server decides which of your data goes into the prompt. Your health journal, body records and plans are included only for the trainer and nutritionist. The keeper gets your streak counts only (days in a row, and whether today is logged yet), never the values behind them. Your "inner context" is included only for counsel, trainer and nutritionist.
  • No third-party AI provider. Server chat, vision and speech-to-text go to Runink's own services. Free-tier chat runs on your phone. Spoken replies use the phone's own voice (see the table below).
  • Conversation text is not routinely logged. Server logs don't routinely contain your messages. Your email address is logged at sign-in.

Third parties the apps contact

The model side is fully sovereign. The client still reaches a few outside services:

Contact What it receives When
Google Sign-In Your identity at sign-in Always
The device speech recogniser (usually Google's on Android) Your voice Only on the STT fallback path
The device text-to-speech engine The text of Luna's replies Every spoken reply
OpenStreetMap Your IP address and the map area you view While tracking an activity
GitHub (raw content) Your IP address and which exercise image When exercise images load
Open-Meteo Coordinates coarsened to about 1 km Only if you connect local weather

Health Connect is not on this list. It is on-device, and Luna only reads from it.

Honesty markers you will see

  • "not saved — Luna won't remember this": a reply that streamed but couldn't be written to the journal. The conversation carries on, but that turn won't be recalled later.
  • "not verified": a model proposal that could not be confirmed.
  • A dash or "not reported": a value Luna doesn't have. It is never shown as zero.

Deletion

Note: Deletion is on request. Ask Runink, as the privacy policy describes. Changing or removing a record, such as a pantry item or your inner context, may leave earlier versions in place until they are deleted on request.

Records are kept until they are deleted on request; there is no automatic retention window. Removing an account's approval revokes all of its sessions immediately, and its data stays in place until a deletion request.
