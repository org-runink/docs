Training plans

Training plans

Note: Building, revising and reading plans runs on the LUNA backend, so on the free tier it is part of the paid plan, which is coming. A plan you already saved stays readable, and logging sets follows the free tier's 14-day rule.

Training is the trainer aspect's domain. A plan is built with you, reviewed before anything is live, and then followed on the Training card every day.

Plans use real exercises

Plans are built from real exercises, each with its target muscles, equipment and real step-by-step instructions.

Each plan also shows:

  • Weekly hard sets per body part (weekly_sets_by_body_part).
  • How the plan progresses, in one line.
  • The rationale and a status note that echoes any constraint you gave ("left shoulder injury — pressing suspended").

Four ways to get a plan

Route How Model involved?
Ask in chat "make me a 4 day workout plan". Days ("4 days", "3x") and session length ("45 min") are taken from what you wrote. No. The plan is shown as a draft deck to approve.
Design it on the plan screen ⋯ → Training plan, then fill in the goal, days per week, session length, experience, equipment you actually have, and anything to design around. Tap Design my plan. No (DraftPlan).
Revise it the button to ask Luna for a change, for example "swap the deadlifts, my back is sore". Yes (RevisePlan). The result is judged before you see it.
Rebuild from photos Attach → Rebuild my plan from a document (a PDF on the phone app, or photos of a coach's sheet, a whiteboard, a screenshot). Yes (PlanFromImages). The result is a draft to review.

Nothing is live until you save it. Drafts, revisions and photo readings all come back unsaved. Save as my plan (or Save revision) calls SavePlan, which makes it active. You can keep several saved plans (a winter block, a travel week) and switch between them under My plans without rewriting them (SetActivePlan).

To swap one movement by hand, search from the plan screen ("Search movements — "row", "squat"…"). This uses SearchExercises, and the source attribution is shown with the results.

Loads: a dash until you lift

LUNA never derives a weight from body mass, experience or goal. Each prescribed set says where its load came from (LoadSource):

Source Meaning
unspecified Luna has never been told what you lift for this movement. The app shows a dash, never a number.
bodyweight The movement is a bodyweight movement.
user entered You typed it.
last session Carried forward from what you actually lifted last time.
derived from 1RM Computed from a one-rep max you stated.

The session runner in the plan console is the only way a load enters the system. You work through the session one set at a time, and each set is saved to the server (LogSet) the moment you mark it done. It isn't buffered until the end, so a phone that kills the app mid-workout loses nothing. A logged set records reps, load in kg (0 is a valid bodyweight set), an optional RPE from 1 to 10, a warm-up flag and a note. Next week's prescription is built from these sets.

Unlocked trainer skills

With Periodization unlocked, plans gain week-by-week progression and a deload week. With Conditioning unlocked, training days end with a finisher. A locked skill produces exactly the plan you'd get without the feature. See Levels, traits and skills.

Form review from photos

Attach → Judge my exercise form sends still frames, not video, with the exercise name (ReviewWorkoutForm). One request looks at up to 3 images. If you pick more frames, the app uses the first three and says so.

The critique goes through the judging ladder (below). If the judge dissents, you get Luna's non-recommendation instead of the model's critique.

The judging ladder

Every proposal the model makes that you might act on is checked before it is shown: plan revisions, plans read from photos, meal food choices, meal-photo estimates and form reviews. The verdict travels with the result:

  • Concur: shown as is.
  • Dissent: withheld. For a revision you get your unchanged plan back, and Luna says why.
  • Unable to judge: shown but marked "not verified". This is common for photos, because a text judge can't see the image.

Plans from chat and DraftPlan, and your streaks, points and targets, aren't judged, because no model proposed them. Free chat replies aren't judged either: they stream token by token, so there is no finished proposal to check.

Warning: The trainer is not a doctor or physio. For pain, numbness or a suspected injury, the prompt instructs Luna to tell you to stop and see a professional before any programming. This is prompt guidance, not a medical safeguard.
