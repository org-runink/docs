Overview

LUNA Documentation

Warning: Status: not yet available. LUNA is in development. Android and iOS are the target platforms; nothing has been released to Google Play or the App Store, and the iOS app has never been compiled. The web build exists for testing only. Access is for approved accounts only; there is no open sign-up.

Keeping up a training plan, a meal plan and a step goal takes daily bookkeeping: working out targets, remembering what you did, and noticing when a streak is about to break. Runink LUNA (Lifestyle Understanding & Navigation Assistant) carries that bookkeeping for you, so the only thing left to do each day is check in.

LUNA is a personal companion app. Meet Luna: one named companion on a single screen, who keeps your habit streaks, coaches your training and meals, talks with you by text or voice, looks at photos you share, keeps a record of your pets, and grows as your streaks hold. No third-party AI API is ever called.

Who it is for

One person, on their own phone. Every approved account has its own private journal, memory, plans and streaks with Luna; nothing is shared between accounts. There are no teams, workspaces or shared views.
