Data model

Data model

One journal per account

Everything LUNA keeps about a person is encrypted and private to that account. Nothing is shared between accounts, and a request without an account is refused.

The records

Record Holds
Chat turns What was said, typed or spoken, and which aspect answered
Habit events Workouts, meal adherence, step counts, extra steps, level-ups, the steps goal
Training plans and logged sets Plans of real exercises; each set's reps, load, effort and note
Meal plans and pantry items Targets and example meals; label values you entered
Profile and inner context Body mass, goals, units, preferences, tone; your own baseline, stressors and strategies
Body readings and wearable days Scale readings, sleep and activity summaries from Health Connect
Activities Summaries of tracked walks, runs and rides (never the route)
Pets Typed pet records, weights and vet history
Avatars The chosen form or your uploaded video loops
Memory An index of earlier conversations, for recall

Rules the data model keeps

  • No invented health data. Steps come from the phone, loads from logged sets, nutrition numbers from your own entries or from a label you entered.
  • Honest persistence. A reply that could not be saved is marked "not saved".
  • Data minimisation. Each aspect is given only the records it needs.
