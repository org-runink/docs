Services

Services

Every service lives in the gRPC package luna.v1 and needs a session, except signing in. See the API concepts.

Service What it does Main calls
IdentityService Google sign-in for approved accounts, who am I (including the plan), sign out GoogleLogin, WhoAmI, Logout
AvatarService Luna's form: a drawn form or your own video loops, and which one is selected CreateAvatar, ListAvatars, SelectAvatar, GetSelectedAvatar
ChatService Streamed chat turns, the plan actions in chat, confirmations, history SendMessage, ListHistory
VoiceService Server speech recognition and speech Transcribe, SynthesizeSpeech
VisionService Meal photos, nutrition labels, form review from frames or video, paper documents, animals and plants, document guidance AnalyzeMeal, ReadNutritionLabel, ReviewWorkoutForm, ReviewExerciseVideo, AnalyzeSubject, CoachDocuments
TrainingService Training plans, the movement search, set logging, insights and export DraftPlan, RevisePlan, SavePlan, ListPlans, SetActivePlan, SearchExercises, LogSet, ListSets, GetTrainingInsights, ExportSets, PlanFromImages
NutritionService Meal plans that follow the training week, and the pantry DraftMealPlan, SaveMealPlan, ListMealPlans, SetActiveMealPlan, ListPantry, SavePantryItem, DeletePantryItem
HabitService Habit events, the score and streaks, levels, the steps goal, tracked activities LogEvent, GetScore, LevelUp, SetStepsGoal, RecordActivity, ListActivities
ProfileService Profile, body readings, wearable days, inner context, your own conscience checks, today's deeper-conversation budget GetProfile, UpdateProfile, RecordBodyComposition, RecordWearableDay, GetInnerContext, UpdateInnerContext, GetConscienceTrace, ListConscienceTraces, GetChatBudget
PetService The person's typed pet records and the vet sheet ListPets, SavePet, AddWeight, AddVetEvent, GetVetSummary

On the free tier, the calls that run a server model (chat, server speech recognition, every vision call, and drafting or revising plans) are refused with a plain reason. See Free tier and paid plan.
