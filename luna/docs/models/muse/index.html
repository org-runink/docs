      Muse

      Muse

      What it does

      The muse helps with creative work by offering distinct directions instead of one safe answer.

      • Creative and artistic work: writing, design, music, naming.
      • Getting unstuck on a project.
      • Honest critique when you ask for it.
      • With the Wilder Palette skill unlocked, it offers stranger, more divergent directions first.

      What it reads

      • Your message and the conversation so far.
      • Long-term memory of earlier conversations, such as a project you mentioned.

      It does not read your health journal, body records, plans or inner context. A creative question has no business carrying a health record.

      What it produces

      A conversational reply, as text or read aloud: usually a few labelled directions you can pick from and push further.

      Human oversight

      • You choose. It suggests; what you use is up to you.

      Model

      Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, by Qwen, licensed Apache-2.0. Runink domain adaptation for this agent is planned; this release uses the base model.

      Where it runs and data handling

      On Runink-operated servers, not on your phone. No third-party AI service is called. On the phone apps, spoken replies are read aloud by the phone’s own speech engine. See Privacy and data.

      Guardrails

      • Original work only: no song lyrics or substantial copyrighted passages. Pastiche is fine when it’s named as such.
      • It won’t write content that targets a group.
      • The Safety rules apply to every chat turn.

      Limitations

      • It produces text only. It doesn’t make images or audio.
      • It works from what you describe or paste into the chat; it can’t open your files.

      Evaluation

      No published evaluation scores yet.

      Illustrative example

      Invented data.

      You: I’m stuck naming my story collection.

      Luna (muse): Three directions. Plain: “Night Watch”. Mood first: “What the Fog Kept”. Stranger: “The Inventory of Gulls”. Pick a lane and I’ll give you more in it.
