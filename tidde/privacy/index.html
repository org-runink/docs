Runink TIDDE — Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Runink TIDDE console, on the web and, once Runink publishes it, as the Android app (application id org.runink.tide; not yet available), and the TIDDE deployment it connects to. For the rest of the Runink platform, see the general privacy policy.

TIDDE is a console for a deployment that you or your organisation run. The app is a window onto that deployment. We do not host a deployment you run, and we do not receive your data through the app. During a trial, or when you use a shared instance that Runink runs, your workspace is hosted by Runink, and Runink holds its data on your behalf.

Who can use TIDDE

TIDDE is not open to the public. An administrator of your organisation's deployment adds you. The app has no sign-up form.

What TIDDE handles

Your account

  • Sign-in. You enter the address of your deployment, then your user name and password. If your deployment offers Google sign-in, you can use that instead. The app sends only Google's signed identity token to your deployment, which checks it. We never see your Google password.
  • Your account record. Your deployment holds your email address and your role. The app shows your email address.
  • Your session. Your deployment sets it, and it ends after 12 hours by default. Signing out ends it.

What you do in TIDDE

The questions you ask, the chat text you type, the commands you run and the notes you save go to your deployment and are kept there. On the web, you can also choose a file to import (for example, a spreadsheet). The file goes to your deployment.

On your device

The console keeps a few interface choices: your language, light or dark theme, the last instance you chose, and whether the side rail is collapsed. On the web, these live in your browser's local storage. Clear your browser data, or the app's data on Android, to remove them.

Microphone

The web console can use the microphone for voice commands, and only when you press the voice control. The recording goes to your deployment, which turns it into text. The Android app does not ask for the microphone.

What TIDDE does not collect

  • No advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service in the app. It contains no Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry or similar library.
  • No location, contacts, calendar, messages, photos or device identifiers. The Android app asks for one permission, internet access.
  • No backup of app data. Android backup and device transfer are switched off for the app.

Where your data goes

The app talks to one server: the deployment whose address you entered. It connects over an encrypted connection to the instance you enter. We do not receive what you send. The owner of your deployment is responsible for that data under their own policy, and the general policy covers what Runink handles for your organisation's account.

The one outside party the app can contact is Google, for sign-in, and only when you choose Google sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies to that step.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstall the app, or clear its storage.
  • On your deployment: ask your administrator to delete your account or the data it holds.
  • Anything Runink can do: write to the contact below.

Children

TIDDE is a business console for administrators. It is not meant for children.

Changes to this policy

We post changes on this page and update the date below.

Your rights and complaints

Runink is published by Technologies Runink Inc., Montréal, Québec, Canada.

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to give you a copy. For privacy requests, email contact@runink.org. We read that mailbox, and we answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

This policy is governed by the laws of Québec and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
