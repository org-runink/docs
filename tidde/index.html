Runink TIDDE Documentation

Runink TIDDE documentation: overview, concepts, architecture, and the User, Admin and Developer guides.

Runink TIDDE (Trusted Intelligence for Developer & Data Experience) is the console and platform under the Runink applications: clusters and servers, data sources and runners, the agents that review, fix, audit and plan, and FORGE, the studio that builds apps and pipelines from an approved chat.

  • Overview: What TIDDE is, who uses it, and what is available today.
  • Concepts: Goals and plans, autonomy, the judge, the harness, approved accounts.
  • Architecture: The components and how they fit together.
  • User guide: Every page of the console: Overview, AGENTS, DEVEX, DataEx, Intelligence, FORGE.
  • Admin guide: Access and roles, connections, runners, personas, the trial.
  • Developer guide: APIs, the CLI, MCP tools, and building on TIDDE.

Prices and plans are on the Runink TIDDE product page.
