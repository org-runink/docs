Trial and subscription

Trial and subscription

This page covers what an admin needs to know about the trial and the subscription. Prices and plans are not in this guide: they are on the Runink TIDDE product page. TIDDE is sold separately from FACE, PULSE and LUNA, and FORGE is part of TIDDE.

The trial

  • A trial lasts 14 days, and every trial is the same.
  • A trial is subject to approval like any other access: Runink approves the organisation and adds its first admin before anyone signs in. Creating an account on the sign-in page does not give access.
  • To continue after the trial, contact Runink. When the trial ends, the workspace becomes read-only, with no data lost, until a plan is bought.

Note: An expired trial becomes read-only once the update that provides it reaches your installation; earlier builds still close the pages.

The Billing section of Settings

Settings › Billing shows your subscription and its invoices from the payment provider, when the installation has one configured. Without one, it says billing is not configured. A value TIDDE does not measure is shown as not measured, never as zero, and an estimate is never shown as a charge.

Access after the trial

After the 14-day trial the workspace is read-only until a plan is bought. Who may use an instance, and with which role, is set on Access and roles.
