Sign in

Sign in

This page takes you from the sign-in screen to your instance on the Overview page.

Before you sign in: access is by approval

TIDDE is not open to the public. Sign-in is for approved accounts only. Creating an account on the sign-in page does not give access: your address must be on the approved list. You are approved in one of three ways:

  • Admins are added by Runink.
  • Members are added by an admin of the instance, on Admin › Members.
  • An approved domain: an installation can instead admit every verified address of one approved domain.

If your address is not on the list, sign-in is refused. An empty list refuses everyone: the check fails closed.

Step 1: sign in with Google

Open your installation's console URL and choose Sign in with Google. TIDDE verifies the Google ID token and requires a verified email address on the approved list.

Each installation also has a break-glass administrator sign-in for its installer, described in the private install guide. It is not for day-to-day use.

Sessions are kept on the server. Log out in the rail's account block ends your session on the server, and Sign out everywhere (Settings › User) ends all of them. A session expires on its own after 12 hours; the sign-in page then says so and returns you to where you were.

Step 2: choose an instance

After sign-in you land on Overview. Its bird's-eye shows every instance and application control plane you may see. Drill into one to put it in focus. Admin appears in the rail footer only when the server reports that you administer the instance in focus; every access call checks the role again on the server.
