      Campaigns and results

      Campaigns and results

      The services behind Campaigns (the board, campaign pages, plans, approvals and Autopilot) and the measured results. Package pulse.v1; the least role of each method is in its table. See Autopilot and agencies.

      Notes

      • Measured only. Metrics come from connected sources: Google Analytics 4 as channel web, Google Ads as google_ads, Google Search Console clicks and impressions, and the leads PULSE holds. Published posts have their own results, read back into post_results. A channel with no connector has no rows. A figure is counted only when measured_fields names it; measured_days of 0 means nothing was measured. Nothing writes follower counts.
      • Forecasts. GetForecast splits the cached Google Analytics series into trend, seasonality and changepoints and extends it, without a live Google call and without model inference. season_period_days is 0 when no seasonality was found.
      • Autopilot queues approved posts at the account’s best time through the same publishing gate as any scheduled post, so the switches on Switches and arming order still decide at send time. Replies drafted from read-back comments wait in the approval queue.
      • Automations do outbound work only with PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS and each step’s own channel switch; a dry run never sends.
      • Campaign plans. Go lays out the calendar from the person’s capacity and dates; the campaign-plan skill fills the posts; checks reject plans over capacity or with missing fields. KPI results come only from post_results.

// The state of a campaign plan.
enum CampaignPlanState {
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_UNSPECIFIED = 0;
  // Proposed from an audit's content gaps; waiting for the user.
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_PROPOSED = 1;
  // Accepted; the strategy and roadmap are being prepared.
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_PREPARING = 2;
  // Strategy and roadmap are ready.
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_READY = 3;
  // Preparing failed; detail says why. Accepting again retries.
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_FAILED = 4;
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_DISMISSED = 5;
  // Still a proposal; its strategy and roadmap are being drafted.
  CAMPAIGN_PLAN_STATE_DRAFTING = 6;
}

      ContentPlanService

      ContentPlanService — a campaign as a real content plan (owner, 2026-10-29): campaign › phases (dated, around an optional event) › posts (a calendar row each: date, format, topic, persona, hook, call to action, posting time in the account’s time zone), a Stories plan per day on Instagram, a tone of voice per persona and three KPIs.

      The rules a reader can rely on: * The person states the capacity (posts per week per channel, stories per day) and the dates; PULSE lays the calendar out inside them, keeps the promotional ratio, and posts at the account’s MEASURED best time where results exist, otherwise at a starting time it labels as not measured. * The model proposes topics, hooks and personas through the campaign-plan skill (TOON); Go checks capacity, ratio, dates and duplicates; OpenBias and the plan gate judge the plan. A withheld plan is not saved. * Personas come from the account’s audit and leads, never invented. * KPI targets are proposals; KPI results are measurements read from the account’s published posts, absent until something was measured. * Nothing here publishes. A post is written in Studio and approved on the Drafts tab; approval hands it to the existing autopilot schedule and dispatch gate (PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED + per-channel switches).

      pulse.v1.ContentPlanService, declared in content_plan.proto.

      MethodRequest → ResponseLeast roleNotes
      ProposeContentPlanProposeContentPlanRequest → ContentPlaneditorProposeContentPlan lays out (or lays out again) a campaign’s plan from the person’s capacity and dates. Posts already written are kept.
      GetContentPlanGetContentPlanRequest → ContentPlanviewerGetContentPlan returns a campaign’s plan with its KPI results as measured now. NotFound when the campaign has none yet.
      UpdatePlannedPostUpdatePlannedPostRequest → PlannedPosteditorUpdatePlannedPost moves or edits one post (date, time, format, topic, persona, hook, call to action). A date outside the campaign is refused.
      RemovePlannedPostRemovePlannedPostRequest → ContentPlaneditorRemovePlannedPost removes one post that has not been written yet.

      Also uses Channel.

      EpicService

      SERVICE: EpicService — Epics and Milestones (replaces the Strategy Roadmap) An epic is one campaign objective with a measured KPI, an accountable owner (who created it), a named approver and RACI. A milestone is a dated gate with an exit threshold on that KPI and a Definition of Done; the campaign’s roadmap items roll up into milestones. Every figure (KPI, forecast, control chart, schedule index) is computed in Go from stored measurements; a figure that cannot be computed is unset with the reason, never 0.

      pulse.v1.EpicService, declared in pulse.proto.

      MethodRequest → ResponseLeast roleNotes
      ListEpicsListEpicsRequest → ListEpicsResponseviewerListEpics lists the epics the caller owns or approves (one campaign, or all).

No model call; kinds the campaign already has are kept.
      UpdateEpicUpdateEpicRequest → EpiceditorUpdateEpic sets the target, its date, the approver and RACI (owner only).
      SetDoneCriterionSetDoneCriterionRequest → EpiceditorSetDoneCriterion adds, ticks or unticks a Definition-of-Done line (owner).
      ApproveMilestoneApproveMilestoneRequest → EpiceditorApproveMilestone signs a gate off: the named approver only, and only once its exit criterion is met and its Definition of Done complete.
      ListApproversListApproversRequest → ListApproversResponseviewerListApprovers lists the people who may approve (editor or admin role), or everyone (for Consulted and Informed).

      AutopilotService

      AutopilotService — PULSE runs a connected social account by itself AFTER a person approves the content (owner, 2026-10-29: “PULSE agents will autonomously manage our social media accounts once the content is approved”).

      The rules a reader can rely on: * Approval is the human gate. Autopilot schedules only content a person approved, and the publishing worker checks the approval again on the row it is about to send. * PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED and the per-channel PULSE_PUBLISH_(channel) switches are the hard kill switches: autopilot never schedules on a disarmed channel and every send passes the same dispatch gate as a manual one. * An account pauses by itself on an expired connection, a rate limit or repeated failures, and says so in To do today with a reconnect link. * Replies to comments are drafted into an approval queue. Auto-reply is OFF by default and, when a person turns it on, covers only thanks, an answer from approved FAQ content and a link to the business’s own site; each auto-reply passes OpenBias and the plan gate and is logged. * Comments, mentions, DMs and results are read only where the connector provides them; elsewhere the account says PULSE cannot read them yet. * Everything is owner-scoped.

      AutomationService

      AutomationService — Content › Automations: a person’s marketing automations, each ONE trigger and a graph of steps, drawn on the shared flow studio (org-runink/ui flow). The engine is ui’s flow/engine; every step calls an existing PULSE capability with the AUTOMATION OWNER’s identity and RBAC.

      The rules a reader can rely on: * The model PROPOSES; the person APPROVES. The assistant’s propose_automation tool never changes an automation: it stores a pending proposal (plan-gated). Only DecideAutomationProposal(approve) applies it, and only against the graph it was proposed on. * An automation runs by itself only when ARMED (default off). Any change to what it runs disarms it. * A dry run shows what WOULD happen and changes nothing, sends nothing and spends no model time. * Every outbound step (schedule a post, a HubSpot write, an email, a call) also needs PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED=true, PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS and the step’s own channel switch and allowlists. * Everything is owner-scoped: another person’s automation, run, proposal or chat entry is NOT_FOUND.
