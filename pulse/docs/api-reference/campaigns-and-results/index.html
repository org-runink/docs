Campaigns and results

Campaigns and results

The services behind Campaigns (the board, campaign pages, plans, approvals and Autopilot) and the measured results. Package pulse.v1; the least role of each method is in its table. See Autopilot and agencies.

Notes

  • Measured only. Metrics come from connected sources (Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Search Console) and the leads PULSE holds. Published posts have their own results. A channel with no connector has no rows. A figure is counted only when measured_fields names it; measured_days of 0 means nothing was measured.
  • Forecasts. GetForecast extends the stored Google Analytics series. season_period_days is 0 when no seasonality was found.
  • Autopilot queues approved posts at the account's best time through the same publishing gate as any scheduled post, so the switches on Switches and arming order still decide at send time.
  • Automations do outbound work only when your administrator armed them and each step's own channel; a dry run never sends.
  • Campaign plans follow the person's capacity and dates; KPI results come only from measured post results.

ContentPlanService

ContentPlanService — a campaign as a real content plan (owner, 2026-10-29): campaign › phases (dated, around an optional event) › posts (a calendar row each: date, format, topic, persona, hook, call to action, posting time in the account's time zone), a Stories plan per day on Instagram, a tone of voice per persona and three KPIs.

The rules a reader can rely on: The person states the capacity (posts per week per channel, stories per day) and the dates; PULSE lays the calendar out inside them, keeps the promotional ratio, and posts at the account's MEASURED best time where results exist, otherwise at a starting time it labels as not measured. Topics, hooks and personas are proposed by the campaign-plan skill; the plan is checked for capacity, ratio, dates and duplicates and reviewed. A plan that fails the review is not saved. Personas come from the account's audit and leads, never invented. KPI targets are proposals; KPI results are measurements read from the account's published posts, absent until something was measured. * Nothing here publishes. A post is written in Studio and approved on the Drafts tab; approval hands it to the existing autopilot schedule and publishing checks (publishing must be enabled for the channel).

AutopilotService

AutopilotService — PULSE runs a connected social account by itself AFTER a person approves the content (owner, 2026-10-29: "PULSE agents will autonomously manage our social media accounts once the content is approved").

The rules a reader can rely on: Approval is the human gate. Autopilot schedules only content a person approved, and the approval is checked again before each send. Publishing must be enabled for the deployment and for the channel: autopilot never schedules on a disarmed channel and every send passes the same checks as a manual one. An account pauses by itself when something stops it from posting, and says so in To do today with a reconnect link. Replies to comments are drafted into an approval queue. Auto-reply is OFF by default and, when a person turns it on, covers only thanks, an answer from approved FAQ content and a link to the business's own site; each auto-reply is checked and logged. Comments, mentions, DMs and results are read only where the connector provides them; elsewhere the account says PULSE cannot read them yet. Everything is owner-scoped.

AutomationService

AutomationService — Content › Automations: a person's marketing automations, each ONE trigger and a graph of steps. Every step calls an existing PULSE capability with the AUTOMATION OWNER's identity and RBAC.

The rules a reader can rely on: The assistant PROPOSES; the person APPROVES. The assistant never changes an automation: it stores a pending proposal. Only DecideAutomationProposal(approve) applies it, and only against the graph it was proposed on. An automation runs by itself only when ARMED (default off). Any change to what it runs disarms it. A dry run shows what WOULD happen and changes nothing and sends nothing. Every outbound step (schedule a post, a HubSpot write, an email, a call) also needs publishing enabled for the deployment and the step's own channel enabled and allowed. * Everything is owner-scoped: another person's automation, run, proposal or chat entry is NOT_FOUND.

GrowthPlanService

GrowthPlanService — the 90-day plan (owner, 2026-11-06: "qualified inbound leads and roadmap automation plans, road map and action plans for 90 days"). The spine of the console: made from the latest check-up, three 30-day phases (fix and foundations, publish and attract, convert and scale), weekly actions.

The rules a reader can rely on: Every action comes from one finding of the check-up (a site-score fix, an AI-access failure, a buyer question the AI answers without the business, a missing Discover or retention item, a content gap's campaign item, a lead the check-up found). An action without its finding is never made. The plan is laid out from what was measured. Editing (accept, drop, move, owner) changes only the plan. Nothing the plan links to is created by the plan: a piece is still written and approved on the board, an email still waits for its send, a fix still goes through the site report. KPIs are computed when read, from measured baselines only. A KPI with no source reads "not measured", with the step that would measure it.
