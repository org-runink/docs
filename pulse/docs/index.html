Overview

Overview

Runink PULSE: Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine. Your marketing agency in an app. A small business loses its week to working out what to post, where and for whom, then writing it, posting it, answering comments, chasing leads, and checking whether any of it worked. PULSE does that work, and you approve what goes out.

You give it your website. A check-up reads your site and the web and shows, on Brand Analysis, how your business looks online: your site score, whether AI answers and Google name you, your competitors, partners and leads. From that, PULSE makes a 90-day plan and campaigns, writes the posts for your approval, finds leads and public tenders, runs outreach sequences from your own mailbox, and shows what came of it.

PULSE shows only figures it actually measured. A number it could not obtain reads Not measured, never 0 and never an estimate. Every AI step runs on the platform's own models; PULSE calls no outside AI service.

Availability

Warning: What is built and what is not yet available.

  • PULSE is an Android app. An iOS app is planned and not yet built. There is no web app.
  • PULSE is not yet available on Google Play, the App Store or any cloud marketplace. Runink installs it for approved organisations.
  • Sign-in is Google only, for accounts Runink approved. There is no open sign-up. Roles (viewer, editor, admin) come from your organisation's subscription on Runink's TIDDE platform; admins are added by Runink.
  • Everything that goes outward (publishing, email, calls, messages, the website chat) is off by default and stays off until an administrator arms it and a person approves each action.
  • Google Business Profile and reviews are built but not yet available: Google has not yet granted API access.

For prices and plans, see the PULSE product page.

PULSE's logo is its persona: the puppy running over a heartbeat line, shown in the corner of these pages and on the app's sign-in screen. Runink is the company that makes PULSE, so the app and these pages say PULSE by Runink.
