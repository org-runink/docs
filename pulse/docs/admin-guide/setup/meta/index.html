Meta (Instagram)

Meta (Instagram)

For the administrator: what to set up so people can press Connect with Instagram under Settings › Integrations. Without it, the Instagram card says sign-in is not set up and accepts a pasted token instead.

In the Meta developer console

  1. Create an app with Facebook Login for Business. PULSE uses the Instagram API with Facebook Login (Graph API v23.0): the person signs in at Facebook, and PULSE finds the Instagram professional account through the Facebook Page it is linked to.
  2. Add <PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL>/oauth/social/callback to the valid OAuth redirect URIs (see Public address and redirects).

  3. Request these permissions. The sign-in asks for all of them at once:

    | Permission | Needed for | |---|---| | instagram_basic, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement | Every feature | | instagram_content_publish | Publishing posts, carousels, Reels and Stories | | instagram_manage_insights | Results (reach, views, likes, comments, saves, shares), and checking an @mention before it is tagged (business discovery) | | instagram_manage_comments | Comments, replies, and posts that tag the account | | instagram_manage_messages, pages_manage_metadata | Direct messages and replies to them |

  4. Every permission needs Advanced Access through Meta App Review, and Meta asks for business verification, before it works for accounts that have no role on the app. Until then, only people with a role on the app can connect.

  5. For direct messages, the Instagram account's setting Allow access to messages must also be on.

What @mentions need

Tagging an account in a post, carousel, Reel or Story is part of publishing (user_tags on the media container): it needs no permission beyond instagram_content_publish. Before tagging, PULSE checks each account with business discovery (GET /<IG user>?fields=business_discovery.username(…)), which needs instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights and pages_read_engagement, all already in the sign-in. Business discovery finds only business and creator accounts: a personal account, or any account when instagram_manage_insights is missing, is shown as not checked in Studio and stays in the caption as @name without being tagged (the person decides). Instagram allows 20 @mentions in one caption.

In PULSE

Set META_APP_ID and META_APP_SECRET (the secret from the instance's secret store, never from a repository secret) and an https PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL.

PULSE exchanges the sign-in for a long-lived token, stores it sealed, and renews it in its last ten days; a failed renewal is shown on the card. A permission the person did not grant is listed on the card, and a read that needs it says Reconnect to allow …. Disconnect removes PULSE's grant at Meta (DELETE /me/permissions), then the stored tokens.

Connecting publishes nothing. Publishing also needs PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED and PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM; replies need PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_REPLIES or PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_DMS. See Switches and arming order.

So, for Instagram: Runink PULSE never comments on another account's post. There is nothing to arm: the Approvals page offers Draft an Instagram comment to post by hand (the person pastes the post's link and text, PULSE drafts words, the person posts them in Instagram), labelled "Instagram does not allow apps to comment on other accounts", with no send button.

What PULSE reads on the account's own posts

  • Comments, followed by cursor (paging.cursors.after, 50 per page) up to PULSE_ENGAGEMENT_MAX_PAGES pages (default 4, at most 10) per post, newest first, stopping at the first page older than the window. The next-page URL is never followed: PULSE builds the request itself.
  • The window: PULSE_ENGAGEMENT_WINDOW_DAYS days back (default 14, at most 60) for the posts PULSE sent, mentions and direct messages.
  • Mentions: the media that tag the account (/tags, paged the same way). Caption @mentions are not read. Story mentions are read only through the Meta webhook, when an administrator has armed it (see below).
  • Direct messages: the Page's conversations (20 a page, paged), answered only inside Meta's 24-hour window.
  • A permission Meta refuses (error codes 3, 10 and 200 to 299) is shown as Reconnect to allow …, the same words as on YouTube.
  • Spam under a post (a link that is not the account's own site together with an offer, or three or more links) is proposed for hiding in Approvals: a person approves it, it passes the same switches, My rules and conscience check as a reply, and only then is POST /<comment> (hide) called. It can be shown again in the Instagram app. YouTube's equivalent hides permanently from PULSE (rejected; the API cannot restore it), which the proposal says. Nothing is hidden without a person.

Story mentions, story replies and comment events: the Meta webhook

Instagram sends story mentions and story replies to apps only by webhook (a call Meta makes to PULSE when something happens). PULSE answers that call on one route, /meta/webhook, which does not exist until an administrator arms it.

What it does. An event Meta reports becomes an ordinary item in Campaigns › Approvals, exactly as if PULSE had read it by polling: the same categories, drafts, My rules and conscience check, switches and trace. The webhook only tells PULSE sooner. It never sends anything, and a reply to a story mention or a story reply is an Instagram direct message, so it keeps Meta's 24-hour window. The kinds it acts on:

Event What PULSE keeps Becomes
A story mention (a message with a story_mention attachment) the sender's id, the message id and a fixed line, "Mentioned you in their story." a direct-message item
A story reply (a message that replies to a story) the sender's id, the message id and the words a direct-message item
A direct message as the polled path keeps it a direct-message item
A comment on the account's own post the comment id, the post id and the words a comment item

Everything else Meta may send (reactions, read receipts, edits, echoes of PULSE's own messages, shared posts, pictures with no words, comment replies, caption mentions, live comments) is ignored and only counted. The story's own picture or video is never requested or stored: the address in the payload is not read, so the webhook cannot be made to fetch anything.

Nothing goes out by itself. Auto-reply keeps its own rules, unchanged: off by default per account, and never for a story mention (it is not one of the three allowed kinds). A reply still needs the kill switch, the channel switches, My rules and the conscience check, and is traced.

Setup, in this order.

  1. In the Meta app, add the Instagram webhooks product and note the App Secret (App settings › Basic). Choose a verify token: a long random value.
  2. Create the Secret meta-webhook-secrets in the PULSE namespace with two keys, verify-token and app-secret. They are mounted read-only as files in /etc/runink/meta-webhook/ (deploy/gx10/SECRETS.md) and are never read from an environment variable, never logged and never shown in the app.
  3. Set PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL (https) and PULSE_META_WEBHOOK=on, then restart. The route is registered only when the switch is on and both files are present; otherwise /meta/webhook answers 404 as if it did not exist.
  4. In the Meta console, enter the callback URL that Settings › Integrations shows under the Instagram card (<PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL>/meta/webhook) and the same verify token. Meta then calls PULSE once to verify it.
  5. Subscribe the app to the fields comments and messages. Meta's own pages say which permission and which Instagram API setup each field needs; PULSE does not claim more than they say.

Not done, and not claimed. Every permission still needs Advanced Access through Meta App Review (and business verification) before it works for accounts with no role on the app. This release does not apply for it and does not assume it was granted. The webhook shapes PULSE reads come from Meta's published pages and were not checked against live traffic; read the status line on the card after the first real event.

What PULSE checks on every call.

  • The handshake answers only a request whose hub.verify_token equals the verify token (compared in constant time) and echoes the challenge only then.
  • A notification is accepted only with a valid X-Hub-Signature-256: the HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with the App Secret, compared in constant time before the body is read as JSON. Missing or wrong is answered 403, and the log line says so without any value.
  • The body is capped (PULSE_META_WEBHOOK_MAX_BODY, 256 KiB by default) and parsed strictly: one JSON value, unknown fields ignored.
  • An event id is remembered for PULSE_META_WEBHOOK_DEDUPE_HOURS (48 by default, at most 168; Meta retries for up to 36), so a replay or a retry is dropped; an event older than that window is dropped as stale.
  • Each address and the verified traffic as a whole have a rate limit. PULSE answers 200 at once and works through a bounded queue; when it is full the event is dropped and counted, and Meta is never kept waiting.
  • An event is matched to an account by the Instagram business account id in the notification (entry.id) against the connected integrations. No match, or two PULSE users claiming one account, means the event is ignored and counted; the answer is the same as for a match, so nothing reveals which accounts exist.

What the administrator sees. /health has a meta_webhook block: whether it is configured, the time of the last event and counts by outcome (ok, bad_signature, unmatched_account, duplicate, queue_full, and a few more), counts only. The Instagram card in Settings › Integrations shows a status line from recorded facts, such as "Webhook not configured: needs the app secret and the verify token", and the callback address as text. The verify token's value is never displayed.

Until the webhook is armed, the app says plainly that story mentions and story replies are not read, and every other kind keeps working by polling.
