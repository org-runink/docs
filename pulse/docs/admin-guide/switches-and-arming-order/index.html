Switches and arming order

Switches and arming order

Runink PULSE: Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine ships with every outward action switched off. This page lists each switch in one place: what it defaults to, what it does, and the order to turn them on safely. The detail behind each one is on Publishing controls and Users and access.

These are set by Runink on your deployment; ask Runink to change one.

Yes and no values

The keys do not all parse yes and no the same way, and the difference matters:

  • PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED arms only on the exact value true. yes, on, 1, and TRUE leave it off, and the server warns about it at startup.
  • PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>, PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS, PULSE_AUDIT_APPLY, PULSE_OUTBOUND_CALLS and PULSE_AI_CALLS accept 1, t, true, y, yes, on and 0, f, false, n, no, off, in any case. Anything else, including empty, counts as unset, which for the last four means off.
  • AUTH_GOOGLE_ONLY is on only for the exact value true. PULSE_GRPC_REFLECTION is on for true in any case.
  • SELF_HEAL_AUTONOMOUS and PULSE_OPPORTUNITIES_DISCOVERY default to on, so they read the other way round: they take the same tokens, and unset or unparseable leaves them on. Only an explicit no turns them off.
  • PULSE_LIPSYNC is on for on, true, 1 and yes (any case); anything else, including empty, is off.
  • PULSE_IMAGEGEN reads the same way: on, true, 1 and yes (any case) are on; anything else, including empty, is off.
  • PULSE_JUDGEMENT is on unless set to off (any case).
  • PULSE_RERANKER is on unless set to off (any case).
  • LINKEDIN_COMPANY_PAGES is on for 1, t, T, true, TRUE or True; anything else, including yes, leaves it off.
  • PULSE_WHATSAPP_AUTOREPLY takes the same tokens and is off unless set to an explicit yes.

The switches

Key Default Effect
PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED off The global kill switch for every outward channel, the first half of the audit-apply gate, and what starts the daily site-audit engine. While it is off, nothing publishes, and approving a draft does not queue it at its planned date.
PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL> follows the kill switch Narrows one channel: BLOG, PODCAST, AUDIOBOOK, YOUTUBE, INSTAGRAM, SPOTIFY, LINKEDIN, EMAIL, WHATSAPP, SMS. Can only turn a channel off.
PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM follows the kill switch Approving (or a schedule or Autopilot reaching) an Instagram piece publishes it by what it is: a post (one photo, with caption, hashtags and alt text), a carousel (its rendered 1080×1350 slides), a Reel (the owner's own video, attached in Studio; MP4/MOV, 3 s to 15 min, at most 300 MB and 1920 px wide, index at the start) or Stories (each attached photo or video, 3 to 60 s; no caption, and stickers cannot be posted through the API). A Reel or Story with no video of the owner's gets one made by PULSE at approval (or on download), and so does a YouTube Short (9:16, at most 3 min, uploaded through PULSE_PUBLISH_YOUTUBE; the owner's own video goes first). Reels made by PULSE are at most 90 s and Stories are split into parts of at most 60 s; Instagram's API publishes no stickers, so a Story's stickers are listed on a checklist to add by hand. Without speech the video is silent and says so; a server that cannot make video says "video is not available on this server". A piece without its media is refused for scheduling and withheld at approval with "needs a video / photo: upload one in Studio". Instagram fetches every file from PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL at an unguessable, signed address that expires within two hours; without that URL the channel is not ready. Before publishing PULSE reads the account's content_publishing_limit; at the limit the post moves to after the 24-hour window with the reason. Mentions go in the caption as @name; each one is looked up again with business discovery right before posting and only a found (business or creator) account is sent in user_tags (a photo at its centre, a Reel or the first Story by username); one that cannot be looked up is not tagged. Needs the Instagram professional account connected with instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish (Meta app review); a missing permission is recorded as "reconnect Instagram to allow …".
META_APP_ID, META_APP_SECRET unset The instance's own Meta app (Facebook Login for Business). With both set, Settings › Integrations shows Connect with Instagram: the person signs in at Facebook, PULSE stores a sealed long-lived token, finds the Instagram professional account through their Page, and renews the token in its last ten days (a failed renewal is shown on the card). The app must allow the redirect PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL/oauth/social/callback. Unset: the card says sign-in is not set up and accepts a pasted token instead.
LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID, LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET unset The instance's own LinkedIn app. With both set (and an https PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL), the LinkedIn card shows Connect with LinkedIn: the person signs in at LinkedIn (openid profile w_member_social), PULSE stores the sealed token and the member, and approved LinkedIn posts use that token. LinkedIn tokens last 60 days; PULSE renews them only when LinkedIn issued a refresh token, otherwise the card shows "reconnect before" the date. The app must allow the redirect PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL/oauth/social/callback. Unset: the card accepts a pasted token instead. PULSE_PUBLISH_LINKEDIN and PULSE_PUBLISHER_USER_IDS still decide whether anything is posted.
LINKEDIN_COMPANY_PAGES off Set true only once LinkedIn approved the app for the Community Management API. The card then offers Connect company pages, which also asks for r_organization_social, w_organization_social and rw_organization_admin, and lists the pages the member administers to post as.
PULSE_PUBLISH_EMAIL follows the kill switch Send on a lead's email draft (SendOutreachEmail): one email, sent when the user taps Send, from that user's own connected mailbox (Gmail, Microsoft 365 or SMTP, see Connections). There is no shared sender, so PULSE_PUBLISHER_USER_IDS does not apply. Off, a send is recorded withheld and the sheet keeps Copy and Open in email. Editors and admins only.
PULSE_PUBLISH_WHATSAPP, PULSE_PUBLISH_SMS follows the kill switch A WhatsApp message or SMS through the shared Twilio carrier: Do it on a lead's next step under Leads › To do today, and Send on a result. Each send needs the person to confirm its preview. Recipients are the contacts an admin added under Settings › Messaging, plus, for SMS only, the person's own open leads whose phone number has a recorded source (see below). Off, the send is refused as "not configured" with the switch named; nothing is sent. Calls are not affected.
PULSE_PUBLISH_LINKEDIN_REPLIES, PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_REPLIES, PULSE_PUBLISH_YOUTUBE_REPLIES follows the kill switch Replies PULSE sends from the Autopilot reply queue (Campaigns › Approvals): an answer to a comment (and, on Instagram, to a post that tags the account). A reply needs this switch and the channel's own PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>, so false here keeps posting on and answering off. Off, an approved reply is recorded withheld with the switch named; nothing is sent.
PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_DMS follows the kill switch Answers to Instagram direct messages from the reply queue. Needs PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM too. Independently of every switch, a message is answered only within 24 hours of the person's last message (Meta's window); after that the reply is withheld with that reason.
PULSE_PUBLISH_YOUTUBE_COMMENTS off (unlike the other switches, the kill switch alone does not arm it) A comment on someone else's YouTube video, posted as the account's own channel, only after a person approved the suggestion in Campaigns › Approvals. Needs true here and PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED and PULSE_PUBLISH_YOUTUBE, and the account's own switch (Settings › Account › Suggest comments on other people's videos, default off). Off, an approved comment stays recorded withheld with this switch named; nothing is sent. There is no automatic option: no setting makes PULSE post one without a person. See Google.
PULSE_AB_MIN_SENDS 30 The subject test of outreach sequences (it only exists once a person writes a second subject): how many emails each subject needs before the Subject test card shows a comparison (1 to 100000). Below it the card shows the counts only. PULSE never picks or switches a winner. Opens are not measured.
PULSE_YT_COMMENT_DAILY_CAP, PULSE_YT_COMMENT_CHANNEL_COOLDOWN_DAYS 3, 14 Per account: how many comment suggestions are staged and how many are posted in 24 hours (1 to 10), and how many days a channel is left alone after a suggestion on one of its videos (1 to 90). Both hold again when a person approves: past the cap the comment waits in the queue with the reason.
PULSE_YT_QUOTA_DAILY_BUDGET 8000 Units of the YouTube Data API's daily quota (10,000 by default per Google Cloud project) PULSE may spend in this process: 1 per read, 50 per comment or hide. A call that would go over is not made. Counted in memory by Pacific-time day (when Google resets), so it is not shared across replicas or kept over a restart; keep it under the project's quota with room for other tools. At most 10,000.
PULSE_ENGAGEMENT_WINDOW_DAYS, PULSE_ENGAGEMENT_MAX_PAGES 14, 4 How far back Autopilot reads comments, mentions and direct messages on the account's posts (1 to 60 days), and how many pages it follows per post or inbox in one pass (1 to 10; 50 Instagram comments or up to 100 YouTube comment threads a page). A value that is unset, zero, negative or not a number counts as the default; one above the maximum counts as the maximum. YouTube pages cost 1 unit each.
PULSE_META_WEBHOOK off Arms the secure Meta webhook (GET and POST /meta/webhook) that brings Instagram story mentions, story replies, comments and direct messages into Campaigns › Approvals as soon as Meta reports them. The route is registered only when this is on and both files of the optional Secret meta-webhook-secrets (verify-token, app-secret, mounted under /etc/runink/meta-webhook/) are present; otherwise it answers 404. It sends nothing: every reply keeps the kill switch, the channel switches, My rules and the conscience check. See Meta (Instagram).
PULSE_META_WEBHOOK_MAX_BODY, PULSE_META_WEBHOOK_DEDUPE_HOURS 262144, 48 The largest notification body the webhook reads, in bytes (4096 to 1048576), and how many hours an event id is remembered so a retry or replay is dropped (1 to 168; Meta retries for up to 36). A value that is unset, zero, negative or not a number counts as the default; one above the maximum counts as the maximum.
PULSE_META_WEBHOOK_SECRETS_DIR /etc/runink/meta-webhook The directory the two secret files are read from. Only a test or an unusual mount changes it.
PULSE_PUBLISH_GBP follows the kill switch Approving a Google Business Profile post (update, offer, event) publishes it on the managed location it names, with the account's own Google sign-in. Not connected, or a location PULSE no longer manages, is withheld with the reason.
PULSE_PUBLISH_GBP_REPLIES follows the kill switch A reply to a Google review, posted only when a person approves it (a negative review also needs the person to confirm they read it). Needs PULSE_PUBLISH_GBP too, so false here keeps posts on and review replies off. Off, the approval is recorded withheld with the switch named.
PULSE_EMAIL_DAILY_LIMIT 50 Emails one user may send in any 24 hours, to protect their mailbox's reputation. A positive whole number; anything else means 50.
GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID, GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET unset The instance's own Google OAuth client. Also used for sign-in and token refresh. Needed for Connect Gmail, which asks for gmail.send and, to see replies, gmail.readonly. Also Connect with YouTube (asks for youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl, yt-analytics.readonly; redirect PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL/oauth/social/callback must be allowed on the client). Unset: the YouTube card accepts a pasted token instead. The same client connects Google Search Console (read-only webmasters.readonly, redirect PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL/oauth/search-console/callback); see Google.
MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID, MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET, MICROSOFT_TENANT_ID unset, unset, common The instance's own Microsoft Entra app registration, for Connect Microsoft 365 (delegated Mail.Send, Mail.Read for replies, User.Read, offline_access). Set the secret from the instance's configuration, never from a repository secret.
PULSE_PUBLISH_CONFIG_PATH, PULSE_PUBLISH_CONFIG_JSON a file set by Runink, none The same per-channel opinions as a file, or inline JSON. An environment key wins over the file.
PULSE_PUBLISHER_USER_IDS empty, which means nobody User ids (as shown in Admin, not emails) allowed to publish through the channels that post as the deployment: LinkedIn, blog, podcast, and audiobook. Anyone else's dispatch is recorded withheld.
Settings › Integrations › Active (per user, not an env var) every channel active until the user saves A channel its owner switched off gets no daily drafts, and its queued posts are recorded withheld: channel not active. It can only narrow: it never arms a channel the switches above leave off.
PULSE_PUBLISH_LINKEDIN follows the kill switch Keep this false to keep LinkedIn drafts-only. See the daily LinkedIn draft.
Settings › Integrations › Autopilot (per account, not an env var) off for every account After a person approves a post, PULSE queues it at the account's best time instead of sending it at once, and the publishing worker sends it through the same gate as any scheduled post: the kill switch, PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>, PULSE_PUBLISHER_USER_IDS and the Active switch above all still decide at send time. Autopilot never queues content nobody approved, and never queues on a disarmed channel (the approval then sends, and is recorded withheld, as before). It pauses the account by itself on an expired connection, a rate limit, or repeated failed sends, and says so under Leads › To do today with a Reconnect link. Turning Autopilot off withholds its queued posts. See Autopilot and agencies.
Autopilot › Answer simple comments by itself (per account) off Replies to comments PULSE reads (LinkedIn, Instagram and YouTube; on Instagram also mentions and direct messages) are drafted into Campaigns › Approvals. A person may let three low-risk kinds go out by themselves: thanks, an answer taken from approved FAQ content, and a link to the business's own site. Each one must pass PULSE's safety rules and the plan gate (with PULSE_JUDGEMENT=off nothing replies by itself) and is logged on its row. A reply is an outbound post on the channel, so it also needs the kill switch, PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>, the channel's reply switch (PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>_REPLIES, or PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_DMS for a direct message), the publisher allowlist and the Active switch.
Settings › Account › Answer vendor pitches (per account, not an env var) off for every account An unsolicited vendor pitch that reaches the account (website form; Instagram direct messages; not LinkedIn, WhatsApp, SMS or website chat, and mail that only arrives in the mailbox only when the account also switched on Read my inbox for vendor pitches, below) is answered with the account's own counter-offer and no per-message approval once the account turns it on and fills in its terms, its Outreach sender name and postal address (and PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL is https). Each answer still needs the kill switch, the channel's own switch (PULSE_PUBLISH_EMAIL for the form's answer; PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM and PULSE_PUBLISH_INSTAGRAM_DMS for a direct message), My rules and the conscience gate, the loop gate, ten answers a day and one per sender. A refused answer waits in the partner pipeline for a person. See Answer vendor pitches.
Settings › Account › Read my inbox for vendor pitches (per account, not an env var; with a sub-option for promotions) off for every account New mail in the account's connected Gmail inbox, read-only, is given to Answer vendor pitches: inbox folder only (promotions, updates, social and forums with the sub-option; spam and sent mail never), only mail received after the switch was turned on, at most 7 days back. Needs Answer vendor pitches on too and gmail.readonly (a restricted Google scope; verification for users outside the project is still to be decided). Gmail only for now. See Answer vendor pitches.
PULSE_VENDOR_INSIGHTS_WINDOW_DAYS, PULSE_VENDOR_INSIGHTS_MIN_PITCHES unset, unset The window in days and the fewest pitches for What vendors pitching us tell us. Either unset: the card shows nothing and names what is missing. Fewer pitches than the minimum: nothing is said. Nothing is sent: a weekly digest is a record in the app. See Answer vendor pitches.
Settings › Account › First response to website enquiries (per account, not an env var) off for every account; the second switch, Send acknowledgements automatically, is also off A new website-form enquiry from a business email address gets a short acknowledgement composed from a template and the account's own words. Switched on, it only prepares a draft that waits for a person's tap; with the second switch on, the clearest cases go without the tap. Nothing happens until the account's Outreach sender name and postal address are filled in and PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL is https. Each send still needs the kill switch, PULSE_PUBLISH_EMAIL, the account's working hours and daily cap, My rules and the conscience gate, the loop gate, and goes at most once per lead. See First response to enquiries.
PULSE_MESSAGE_STEP_DAILY_CAP, and the step Send a WhatsApp or SMS (with your tap) 10; the step is an outbound automation step (needs PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS, the kill switch PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED and the channel's own PULSE_PUBLISH_SMS / PULSE_PUBLISH_WHATSAPP) The most messages a day (1 to 200) the step may make or send per account; past it the step is refused with the reason. The step only MAKES a message: each one waits for a person's tap (Content › Automations › Messages), and only a contact with a consent a person recorded for that channel is messaged. A STOP reply, the account's contact hours and the switches are checked again at the tap. No consent, no message.
Content › Automations › Re-engage a quiet lead (per account, not an env var) off for every account Switching it on installs the automation "Re-engage a quiet lead" disarmed; arming it stays the person's own step. Each morning it takes the leads in stage Contacted that never replied and, for each one whose last email from PULSE is at least the quiet period old (default 14 days, 3 to 90), drafts ONE short follow-up that waits for a person's approval in the lead's sequence. It sends nothing itself: after the tap the email goes through the same send as every sequence email (working hours, daily cap, suppression list, footer with the unsubscribe link, My rules, PULSE_PUBLISH_EMAIL). At most 2 re-engagements per lead ever and 10 drafts a day per account. It stops on a reply, a bounce or an unsubscribe.
PULSE_RESPONSE_TIME_WINDOW_DAYS, PULSE_RESPONSE_TIME_MIN_LEADS unset, unset The window in days and the fewest answered leads for the Response time card on Leads › Inbound. Either unset, or too few leads: it says Not measured, never zero. Nothing is sent. See First response to enquiries.
PULSE_VENDOR_PITCH_RETENTION_DAYS 90 Days before an answered pitch's sender address, company, quoted sentence and draft are erased (codes and stage stay).
PULSE_LINKEDIN_CTA_URL https://runink.org The address the daily LinkedIn draft ends with.
PULSE_AUDIT_APPLY off The second half of the audit-apply gate. With the kill switch, it allows the apply, commit, and pr modes of ApplyAuditActions (audit fixes), and Propose as pull request on the website repository, for administrators only. Off (or the kill switch off) refuses both before anything is read from or sent to GitHub.
Settings › Integrations › Website repository (per account, not an env var) not connected The customer's own site repository on GitHub, with a fine-grained token limited to that repository (Contents and Pull requests: read and write), sealed with the envelope key. An administrator connects it; the card shows connected, missing permission, token rejected, branch not found or unreachable. A proposal is one branch pulse/<audit>-<finding> and one pull request through the GitHub API: PULSE never pushes to the base branch, never merges, never edits .github/workflows/ or .git/, refuses .. paths and links, and caps a proposal at 20 files, 256 KiB each and 1 MiB in all. The plan gate judges the edit before the pull request is opened; a dissent opens nothing.
PULSE_APPLY_ROOT unset, which refuses every mode The only directory an audit fix's repo_path may resolve into. Applies to plan too.
PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS off Lets automations (the automation card a person opens on a lead or a campaign item) do outbound work: schedule a post, write to HubSpot, send email from the owner's mailbox, and place AI calls (which also need PULSE_OUTBOUND_CALLS, PULSE_AI_CALLS and the lead's consent record). Under the kill switch: both must be on, and each step still needs its own channel switch and the publisher allowlist. Each automation must also be armed by its owner; a dry run never sends.
PULSE_OPPORTUNITIES_DISCOVERY on The daily read of open procurement data (EU TED, UK Find a Tender and Contracts Finder, Brazil PNCP, CanadaBuys, and US SAM.gov for accounts that added their own key) and of requests for proposals found by PULSE's crawler, for every account with an offering to match. Reading only: nothing is sent or submitted, and the kill switch does not apply. Off stops the daily read; Find → Public tenders: look now on Leads still reads for the person who presses it. Drafting a response (on request, or the opt-in auto-draft) spends model time on the account's own inference.
PULSE_OUTBOUND_CALLS off One of the switches an AI call needs (with PULSE_AI_CALLS and the kill switch). The caller must also be an editor or admin.
PULSE_AI_CALL_ALLOWED_NUMBERS unset A comma-separated list of E.164 numbers. While it is set, this server places a call (AI call from the Call sheet, a plan, a sequence or an automation, or a Send / Call to a saved contact) only to a number on the list; every other number is refused with a plain reason, and only the last four digits are ever logged. Unset: no restriction. Set it to the test number before the first call and unset it afterwards. GET /health shows call_guard (list set yes/no and its size, the three switches), never a number.
Settings › Integrations › AI calls › Pause all AI calls now (per instance, not an env var) running An administrator pauses every AI call on this instance at once, with no restart, and resumes them later. Read at every call gate and again at the dial (a Send / Call to a saved contact too); a setting that cannot be read refuses the call. A call dialled but not yet answered is hung up; a conversation already running ends when someone hangs up. Each change is an audited record (who, when). GET /health call_guard.calls_pause shows running, paused or unreadable, and call_guard.languages the languages calls can speak: calls speak English until a translated call wording exists, whatever the account language is.
PULSE_AI_CALLS off Allows AI voice calls to leads (AICallService.PlaceAICall, AI calls). Needs PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED=true and PULSE_OUTBOUND_CALLS as well, an editor or admin, Twilio voice settings, PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL (https), and for each lead a recorded consent, a time zone and a drafted call script.
Settings › Integrations › AI calls (per account, not an env var) off The account's switch for AI calls from outreach sequences (Cold and follow-up calls from Leads). Only an administrator changes it. Off: a sequence's call step stays a task for the person and no call plan is drafted. On: the call step drafts a plan that a person must approve, call by call; the call is then placed through AICallService.PlaceAICall, so every switch and guard above still applies. Also per account: AI calls a day, how long transcripts are kept, how long an approval lasts, and the calling hours (inside allowed hours). Nothing about this feature is on by default, and recording is not an option: no audio is kept.

Safe arming order

Turn things on one at a time, and check the startup log after each restart.

  1. Confirm the production gate passes

    With PULSE_ENV=production, the server refuses to start on an insecure configuration. Before setting it, confirm that the current pod logs security config validated. See Operations.

  2. Name who may publish as the deployment

    Set PULSE_PUBLISHER_USER_IDS to the owner's <user-id>, as shown in Admin. Do this before arming, so that the moment the kill switch goes on, only the owner's content can leave on LinkedIn, the blog, or the podcast feed.

  3. Narrow the channels you do not want

    Set PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>=false for each channel that must stay off, including PULSE_PUBLISH_LINKEDIN=false if LinkedIn is to stay drafts-only, PULSE_PUBLISH_EMAIL=false if users must not send email from PULSE, and PULSE_PUBLISH_WHATSAPP=false / PULSE_PUBLISH_SMS=false if nobody may message from PULSE. Narrowing first means arming the global turns on only what you meant.

  4. Set up the sign-in apps, if people should connect with one click

    Set an https PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL routed by the TIDDE edge, then the app clients: META_APP_ID / META_APP_SECRET, GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID / GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET, LINKEDIN_CLIENT_ID / LINKEDIN_CLIENT_SECRET. None of them publishes anything: connecting only stores a sealed token. What each developer console needs is on Setup. Leave LINKEDIN_COMPANY_PAGES off until LinkedIn has approved the Community Management API for the app.

  5. Arm the kill switch

    Set PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED=true. Confirm with Runink that each channel shows as armed in the startup log, and that audit-apply is still off.

  6. Arm server writes, only if you need them

    To let administrators apply audit fixes, set PULSE_APPLY_ROOT to the directory that holds the site checkouts, then PULSE_AUDIT_APPLY=true. Leave both unset to keep the server read-only. The same two switches arm Propose as pull request on a connected website repository (Settings › Integrations › Website repository), which needs no PULSE_APPLY_ROOT: it writes only to a new pulse/ branch on GitHub and opens a pull request for a person to merge.

  7. Arm automations, only if you need them

    Set PULSE_PUBLISH_AUTOMATIONS=true once the channels the automations use are armed and allowlisted. Each automation still runs by itself only after its owner arms it, and every step runs with the owner's role.

  8. Let accounts run on Autopilot, only if you want that

    Autopilot is switched on per account by its owner, under Settings › Integrations › Autopilot. It adds no server key: it sends only through channels you armed above, so a channel you narrowed stays off for Autopilot too. To stop every Autopilot post at once, unset PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED (or the channel's PULSE_PUBLISH_<CHANNEL>): queued posts are then recorded withheld when they come due and are never replayed when you re-arm.

    Two things are separate from Autopilot's switch and off until someone turns them on: suggested YouTube comments on other people's videos (the PULSE_PUBLISH_YOUTUBE_COMMENTS row, off even when everything else is armed) and hiding spam on the account's own posts (it uses the reply switches). Both only ever act after a person approves.

  9. Arm outbound calls, only if you need them

    Set PULSE_OUTBOUND_CALLS=true and PULSE_AI_CALLS=true once the Twilio voice settings are in place and the compliance work for calling real numbers is done. See Telephony and messaging.

To disarm, reverse the order, or simply unset PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED: that stops every channel, the audit-apply modes and the daily site-audit engine at once. It also stops every automation's outbound steps. It does not stop outbound calls, WhatsApp and SMS replies, or self-healing, each of which has its own key.

PULSE_ENV=production

The platform sets PULSE_ENV=production on production deployments. With it set, the server refuses to start, logging refusing to start in production, when:

  • the session key (PULSE_JWT_SECRET) is missing or is one the platform refuses, or
  • CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS is unset or contains *. The backend serves no web bundle, so there is no same-origin exemption: pin the origins in the deployment.

A missing or refused session key stops startup whatever PULSE_ENV says; what production adds is that the CORS problem stops it too, instead of warning.

PULSE_ENV also changes one TLS log line. It does nothing else: no billing, usage counting, or plan behavior reads it, and it does not turn reflection on or off.

Messaging a lead at their own number

Do it on a lead's WhatsApp or SMS step can text the lead's own number, not only a contact an admin added. Every rule below is enforced on the server:

  • SMS only, after a confirm tap. The person sees the exact text and the masked number, and taps to send. It still needs PULSE_APPLY_ENABLED=true and PULSE_PUBLISH_SMS not switched off.
  • Only a number with a recorded source. A phone number counts only when PULSE recorded where it came from: the lead's own web page (the audit), a person typing it into PULSE, your website form, or HubSpot. The Leads page shows the source. A number without a source is never texted.
  • STOP is honoured. A reply of STOP (also STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, PARAR, SAIR, BAJA, ARRET) is recorded for that number. After that, the lead's step no longer offers WhatsApp or SMS, and a message that was already previewed is refused when confirmed. START, YES or UNSTOP lifts the block. When Twilio itself refuses a send because the number unsubscribed (error 21610), PULSE records the same block. For the replies to reach PULSE, set the Twilio number's "A message comes in" webhook to <PULSE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL>/comms/twilio/inbound. PULSE checks Twilio's signature on every request, and the webhook refuses everything (503) while no auth token is set.
  • WhatsApp needs an approved template. Meta only lets a business start a WhatsApp conversation with a template it has approved. The shared carrier sends plain text only, so PULSE never starts a WhatsApp conversation with a lead. Do it says so plainly and offers SMS or email instead. WhatsApp to a contact an admin added works as before.

Checked against the code

Every key on this page is read by the backend or, for AUTH_ALLOWED_EMAILS and PULSE_JWT_SECRET, by the shared security library it uses. The backend also reads these keys, which arm nothing and are listed on the configuration reference: LINKEDIN_API_BASE, LINKEDIN_API_VERSION, LINKEDIN_ACCESS_TOKEN, LINKEDIN_AUTHOR_URN, GOOGLE_TOKEN_ENDPOINT, MESH_ISSUER_URL, MESH_ISSUER_TOKEN_FILE, FFMPEG_PATH and the rest of the storage, inference, telephony and billing settings.

Knowledge export and import (market memory)

Settings › Account › market memory can export what PULSE learned as an Open Knowledge Format bundle and read one back. There is no switch to set: both actions are for a person's own session (an editor or administrator; service accounts and automated runs are refused), the export needs the media store the data export already uses, and the import needs the account's learning switch on. Limits: one export an hour per account, a 1 MiB file, at most 700 files and 4 MiB unpacked, and 300 imported entries per account. The export is sealed in the media store under the account and removed after two hours; the link is the store's signed, short-lived download link, so no new public address exists. Each use is one counts-only line in the log.
