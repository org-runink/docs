      User guide

      Runink PULSE: Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine takes the repetitive work out of a marketing team’s week, and it is built around one loop. You audit a site, PULSE learns the market and audience from it, you create and approve content, and you publish and measure. Then the follow-up cycle feeds what you learned back into the next round.

      This guide follows that loop in four stages. The stages are how these docs are organized. The app’s own navigation is a collapsible rail down the left side that groups the same screens into five sections, shown in the table below. On a narrow screen the rail becomes a drawer.

      1. Audit a domain
      Type “Audit example.com” and PULSE fills every page it can, stage by stage, with sources.
      1. Audit
      Site Audit: relevance scores, the diagnosis, and fixes you can apply.
      2. Audience and market
      Radar research, Customer 360, influencers, and partnerships.
      2. Leads
      Sync, capture, prospect, write outreach, and call.
      3. Content pipeline
      Generate, review, approve, and schedule.
      3. Studio
      Whitepapers, podcasts, shorts, presentations, and more.
      4. Follow up and measure
      30-day cycles, check-ins, metrics, trends, and forecasts.
      Assistant, AI team, and voice
      The chat assistant, your AI team, and voice calls.

      Where each screen lives

      Rail sectionScreens
      OverviewOverview (the landing page), Marketing Console, Site Audit, Insights, Impact Analytics, Content Gaps
      AudienceInfluencers, Customer 360, Persona Profile, Intelligence Console, Leads, Follow-up
      ContentCampaigns, Creatives, Approval, Schedule, Courses
      StudioContent Strategy, Strategy Roadmap, Whitepapers, Podcasts, Shorts, Presentations, Infographics, Reports
      SystemIntegrations, AI Team, Settings

      Below those sections the rail has two more entries: Account and, beside it, Billing. Plan and usage are on Billing only. Account carries your appearance choice — dark, light, or whatever your device uses — and, for an instance administrator, the way into Members and into Messaging.

      A bar above every page shows the latest site audit: the domain, how the run ended, and the reason it gives. Selecting it opens Site Audit. Before your first audit it reads Not run yet. The rail also carries the instance PULSE is acting for and a bell with the recent agent activity.

      The old /metrics and /analytics addresses redirect to Insights.

      The Overview page

      Overview is where PULSE opens. It is one column of sections, in this order:

      SectionWhat it holds
      Action requiredAppears only while something is waiting on the Approval queue
      Key figuresRead from your campaigns, approvals, and analytics
      Latest site auditHow your most recent audit ended, and what to do next
      Intelligence feedWhat PULSE did on your account, newest first
      Channel performancePer-channel results
      Quick actionsNew campaign, generate content, review insights

      A figure PULSE could not read is shown as a dash, never as a zero and never as an estimate. Before your first audit, Latest site audit says so and invites you to run one.

      How the stages connect

      One Site Audit seeds most of the other screens. The quickest way to run it is one line in the assistant, Audit example.com, which runs the whole chain below and shows each stage’s result; see Audit a domain. When the diagnosis finishes, PULSE copies its findings forward, with no extra model calls:

      From the diagnosisTo
      Channel prescription suggestionsDraft content on Approval, then Schedule once approved
      Prescriptions and audience segmentsA draft campaign on Campaigns
      The 4-week planA 30-day cycle on Follow-up, with check-ins
      Document and channel suggestionsCreative briefs on Creatives and a draft course on Courses
      Audience segmentsProfiles on Customer 360
      Competitive and positioning analysisA market summary for the Radar
      The nicheMarket research and up to five new leads, run in the background

      You can also start a stage on its own. Content, creatives, outreach, courses, Customer 360 profiles, and lead prospecting all accept an optional diagnostic_id that grounds the output in a diagnosis. They run without one.

      Languages

      The app ships English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese catalogs. Pick the interface language in Settings. Generated content has its own language, set on each generation request.
