Studio

Studio

Studio (/studio) makes content and keeps what was made. It has two parts: Make something new, a list of everything Studio can make (on the left on a wide screen, folded at the top on a phone), and My Library, what was made, grouped the same way.

Making or opening a piece opens it as a card over the page, never as a page of its own; closing the card brings you back.

What Studio makes

The list comes from your PULSE server, so it shows only what your PULSE can make right now. A format that cannot be made is left out, or shown with the reason (for example Video making is not installed on this PULSE yet).

Make a piece

Pick a format. Its card shows that format's own form and nothing else; Change format swaps it. Studio also opens from other pages with the details filled in: Create on a planned post in Campaigns, Draft on a board card.

Most social and written formats are written by a skill: PULSE writes a draft, checks it, and saves it to your Campaigns board in Drafts to approve. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

The checks

Under What PULSE checked, each check is a small chip: green when it passed, amber when it needs work, grey when it could not be checked (never shown as a pass). Checks depend on the format, for example Opening line, Caption, Sounds human, Slides and Hashtags. Then:

  • if a check failed, PULSE rewrote it to fix what the checks found;
  • PULSE's reviewer (the plan gate) gives its verdict: The reviewer agreed with it, The reviewer could not check it, so it is not approved by the reviewer, or The reviewer is switched off on this server;
  • PULSE also reads the draft against what you gave it (your brief, your figures, your dates and names, and any research it fetched for the draft). The result is one line under the verdict: Checked against what you gave PULSE: every figure, date and name in it is there; 1 sentence states something that is not in what you gave PULSE (the draft is kept as written and the sentences are listed, so you can read them before you use it); or Not checked: it has no figure, date or name that could be compared with what you gave PULSE. "Checked" means only that: it says the draft matches what you gave, never that what you gave is true. "Not checked" is never shown as checked;
  • a draft the safety rules or the reviewer refused is Not shown, with the reason. So is a draft that states figures, dates or names that are not in what you gave PULSE (most of its checkable sentences): PULSE keeps nothing and lists the sentences, so you add the missing facts and ask again. This check is always on; there is no setting for it.

An Instagram carousel shows its slide images, drawn by PULSE, exactly as they would be posted: you approve the slides, not a description of them. Text that does not fit a slide fails its check; it is never cut. Each hashtag shows its size (posts a day: niche, medium or broad), measured from Instagram, or why it was not measured.

A piece in My Library

My Library holds what was made, newest first, filed under its format's group. A piece opens as the product itself: its words, slides, file, video or episode, its checks and its status, and what you can do with it:

  • Edit its words and save them back to the same piece.
  • Approve and Schedule: the same as on the Campaigns board (see Approve and schedule).
  • Automate: its own automation.
  • Download: see below.
  • Make another like this: a new card, prefilled with this piece's title and what it was asked for.
  • How this was made: what you asked for, when PULSE kept it.
  • Delete: it leaves your library and will not be posted.

A PDF opens with Open the PDF, a video with Watch the video, and a podcast plays in place.

Instagram pictures and videos

An Instagram piece needs your own picture or video before it can go out, except a carousel (it posts its slides):

  • Instagram post: Attach your photo (one photo, from upright 4:5 to wide 1.91:1).
  • Instagram Reel: Attach your video (MP4 or MOV, 3 seconds to 15 minutes; upright 9:16 looks best).
  • Instagram Stories: each photo or video you attach becomes one Story, in order. Instagram does not let apps add stickers (polls, questions, links): add those in the Instagram app after it is posted. Mentions need no sticker: PULSE tags the checked accounts under Mentions (below).

PULSE checks the file against Instagram's rules before anything is sent. Attaching publishes nothing.

A video made by PULSE. An Instagram post without a photo of your own is posted as a Reel (add a photo in Studio to post it as a photo). When you approve a Reel or Stories piece without a video of your own, PULSE makes one from the script: the script's lines drawn on upright frames and read aloud by your PULSE's speech service. The piece then says Video made from the script: N s (Stories: N parts, each with Watch part N). With no speech service, the video is made silent and says No voiceover: speech service unavailable. If a video cannot be made, the piece says why; the approval still goes through.

Instagram mentions

An Instagram piece (post, carousel, Reel or Stories) can mention other accounts as @name. The Instagram skills propose mentions only from what PULSE has on record, never a made-up name:

  • Partners you track with an Instagram handle (added by you, or found on a page PULSE could verify);
  • Leads whose notes give their Instagram (Instagram: @name or an instagram.com/name link);
  • Competitors PULSE found, only when you switch on May mention competitors PULSE found before writing;
  • an @name you wrote in what you asked for.

A check refuses any other name in the draft, and its @ is taken away, so it is neither mentioned nor tagged.

The piece shows its Mentions as chips. PULSE looks each account up on Instagram (business discovery, which finds business and creator accounts):

  • checked: Instagram found it. It is written in the caption as @name and tagged when the piece is posted: at the centre of the photo (a post, a carousel's first slide), on the Reel, or on the first Story.
  • not checked: Instagram could not look it up (a personal account, a name that does not exist, no Instagram connection, or a missing permission; hover the chip for the reason). It stays in the caption as @name and is not tagged. Keep it or remove it.

Remove a mention with the chip's ×, add one by typing a username or an instagram.com link, or tap an account listed On record, not mentioned yet (switch on Suggest competitors too to list competitors). Check again on Instagram looks every mention up again. PULSE also looks them up once more right before posting. Instagram allows 20 mentions in one caption.

Download

Download saves an approved, scheduled or sent piece as files you can post yourself, whether the channel is connected or not:

  • An Instagram piece comes as one zip: the pictures (image.png for the image PULSE drew when a photo post has no photo of yours, media-01, media-02 … for your own photos, or slide-01, slide-02 … for a carousel), the video PULSE made (reel.mp4, or story-part-01.mp4 … for Stories) when it is small enough, and caption.txt with the caption, each picture's description and the Mentions (which PULSE tags and which it does not, and why). A video you uploaded yourself is offered on its own, not in the zip.
  • Any other piece comes as one text file (a Markdown file for a blog article) with its title, words, hashtags and call to action.

Or each file lists the files one by one, and anything missing says why (for example an opening line too long to draw on one image).

Publishing

Approving a piece hands it to the schedule. Whether it goes out is decided when it is due, behind several switches, all of which must agree:

  1. The channel is connected and switched Active in Settings → Integrations, and its card says Ready to publish.
  2. Your administrator has publishing switched on for the whole PULSE and for that channel. LinkedIn, the blog, the podcast and the audiobook also need you to be one of your PULSE's named publishers.
  3. With Autopilot on, the post waits for the account's best time.

If one says no, the post is held back (withheld), never sent later without you, and the card says which. A channel PULSE cannot post to, or one you have not connected, says: Not connected, so PULSE will not post it. Download it and post it yourself, or connect it in Settings → Integrations.

Channel What PULSE posts
Instagram Posts, carousels, Reels and Stories
LinkedIn Posts, as you or as a company page you administer
YouTube YouTube Shorts, uploaded to your channel
Website Blog articles, to your site through GitHub
Podcast and audiobook Episodes in PULSE's own podcast feed

Facebook, X, newsletters and the other formats are yours to post: download them. See Drafts, approval and publishing and, for administrators, Switches and arming order.

Reports and PDFs

Report (under Long reads and audio) writes a performance report over a period and the figures you ask it to highlight, as a PDF or a CSV. Guides, study guides, mind maps, timelines and comparison tables are made as PDFs. See Results and reports.
