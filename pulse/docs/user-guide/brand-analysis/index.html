Brand Analysis

Brand Analysis

Brand Analysis (/brand-analysis) is where PULSE opens: how your business looks online, what PULSE did for you, and what to do next. It reads your latest check-up and the accounts you connected. It shows only what was measured; a figure nobody measured says Not measured, never 0.

A new session (after signing in, or opening or reloading the app) starts on Let's run your first audit: the check-up's questions, one at a time (see Sign in and first check-up). View History and Open audit on that page show your earlier check-ups. Once you start or open one, the page below is shown for the rest of the session.

Leads, Campaigns and Studio follow the same rule. Until you start or open a check-up in the session, they show no check-up's leads, drafts, proposed campaigns or pieces, and no page's title line shows an earlier check-up's status. Once you do, they show what that check-up made. What earlier check-ups made is never deleted: it is under From earlier audits on each page, one click away. What you made, rewrote, approved or worked on yourself (a lead you moved past New, a draft you edited) always stays in view.

At the top are New site audit (starts another check-up) and View History (your check-ups). To type or speak a command, use the assistant; see Assistant and voice.

The cards

Below the buttons, every part of Brand Analysis is a card, grouped in the order you act on them. Each card gives its one-line answer from the check-up and a dot:

  • Measured: PULSE has a real answer (it may be "nothing found").
  • Not measured: the check-up did not measure it, is still running, or that part did not finish.
  • Needs a connection: only a connected account can answer it (for Results, Google Analytics or Google Ads).
Group Cards
Your plan Your plan: fixes for your site and posts
Your leads People who could buy from you · How likely your leads are to buy · Customers you might lose
Your market Who you compete with · Where you win and where you lose · Partners to contact · New groups of customers to try
How people see you Can people find you? · What people are talking about · How your business comes across · Ready for Google Discover
Ideas Ideas for what to post
Results Results · Your last check-up

Tap a card to open its page. Every page says which check-up it shows and when it ran, and Brand Analysis takes you back. See The Brand Analysis pages.

Your last check-up, and Needs you now

Side by side on a wide screen, one above the other on a phone:

  • Your last check-up: the site and when it was checked, then either Everything was checked. or how many parts finished (the rest are tried again on the next check-up). See how this was done opens every stage, where each answer came from and the full text of anything that failed. Check again starts a new check-up. Before the first one, this is where it says No check-up yet.

  • Needs you now: one line and one button per thing waiting for you:

    • Your plan: N things to do, with See all;
    • N drafts wait for your review, with Review (opens the drafts on Campaigns);
    • N replies wait for you, with Review (the replies Autopilot drafted).

    When all three were read and none waits, it says Nothing waits for you right now. If one could not be read, it says so rather than claiming there is nothing.

When you look at an earlier check-up, a line above says Showing the check-up of its date, with Show the latest.

The key numbers

One period selector, 7 / 30 / 90 days, sets the numbers below it. Each number shows its change when the earlier period was measured too.

Number Where it comes from
Site score The check-up's score of your site, out of 100
New leads found Leads PULSE found in the period, counted from its own records; tap it to open Leads
Named in AI answers Of the AI answers the check-up read, how many cited your site ("N of M")
Times people saw you Impressions your connected accounts reported in the period

Site score and Named in AI answers appear only once there is a check-up; Times people saw you needs a connected source.

What PULSE did

What PULSE did is counted from what PULSE itself saved, so a 0 here is a real 0, marked Counted. Nothing in it is estimated.

  • Posts published, per channel, drafts approved, outreach messages drafted, follow-up tasks done, and leads contacted or moved on.
  • This week: replies sent (and how many PULSE sent on its own), the results of your posts that the platforms reported, and what goes out next. A result no platform reported says Results of your posts: not measured yet. Email me this sends the week to you from your own connected mailbox.
  • Results measured: impressions, clicks, conversions, engagement and ad spend for the period, with engagement by channel, from what your connected accounts reported, and on how many days of the period they were measured. Without them it says Not measured and offers Open Integrations.

Something PULSE keeps no record of says not recorded. For example, a post sent at the moment you approve it is not yet counted here, and the card says so.

How you are doing over time

How you are doing over time draws a line only for what has measured points in the chosen days, and a line starts once there are two.

Line Source
Site score over time · Named in AI answers over time · Searches where you appear · Google Discover ready Your check-ups, one point each
Engaged visits · Page views Google Analytics
Ad views · Ad clicks Google Ads
Search clicks · Search impressions Google Search Console
People who saw your posts What LinkedIn and Instagram reported for your posts

What was never measured is listed on one line (Not measured yet: …), what is connected but has no measured day yet on another, and each source you have not connected gets one Connect button.

The forecast range under a Google Analytics line is marked Approximate range when fewer than 60 days of history were behind it: the range is still shown, and the real value may fall outside it more often than 1 in 20.

What this check-up measured, and your check-ups

  • What this check-up measured draws your site score as a gauge and every dimension the check-up scored, each out of 100. Grey means not measured, not zero.
  • Your check-ups appears once you have more than one. Tap a check-up to see the whole page as it was then. You can delete one, or Clear all previous check-ups (type your domain to confirm; the latest stays unless you tick it). Deleting removes the check-up's steps, what it found and its AI visibility results; your leads, campaigns, drafts and site-score history stay. Start over removes every check-up and what it made; see Start over.
  • Your material lists each file you gave the check-up as A1, A2 …, with every finding that cites it.

Further down the page

Below the overview are Content ideas for your customers (see Ideas for what to post), short previews of what the check-up found about leads, competitors, partners and new groups of customers, and one collapsed card per research area that has something to show.
