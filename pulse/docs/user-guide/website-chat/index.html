Website chat

Website chat

The website chat puts a PULSE assistant on your own website, for your visitors, to answer questions about your business.

Warning: Off until configured. The website chat answers nothing until your administrator sets it up on the server. Until then Settings › Website chat says "This server cannot serve the website chat yet" and why.

What a visitor sees

A small chat window on your site. It says first that it is an AI assistant. A visitor needs no account and never signs in.

  • To leave their details, a visitor fills in the contact form in the chat. Only that form makes a lead: it lands on Leads › Inbound with the source "website chat". Nothing a visitor types elsewhere becomes a lead.
  • A visitor can ask for a person. That puts an item in your bell and in Visitors who asked for a person.

Set it up

Open Settings › Website chat:

  1. Switch Website chat on.
  2. List your site addresses, one per line (like https://example.com). The widget answers only on those sites.
  3. Optionally write a greeting and pick the widget language.
  4. Save. PULSE makes a site key and shows the snippet: Copy it and paste it before </body> on your site. If your site sends a Content-Security-Policy, allow the address the page names in script-src and connect-src.

New key stops the old snippet at once: paste the new one. Revoke the key stops the widget and turns the chat off.

Visitor chats are kept for a limited time and then deleted; the widget tells the visitor, and the page shows the period.

Visitors who asked for a person

Each request shows the visitor's contact details if they left them and the chat, while it is still kept. Mark handled closes it.

Administrators: the server settings are listed in Switches and arming order.
