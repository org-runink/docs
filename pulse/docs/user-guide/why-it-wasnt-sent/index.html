Why it wasn't sent

Why it wasn't sent

One page that answers "I expected that to go out. Why didn't it?" It lists every outward act PULSE considered that has not gone out: posts and scheduled posts, replies and direct messages, WhatsApp and SMS auto-replies, outreach emails, replies to partner pitches, first-response acknowledgements, AI calls and the questions PULSE asks you. Each row says in plain words what happened, what would change it, and links to the record and to What was checked.

Status: built. Open it from Settings › Account › My rules, beside What PULSE did and why.

What you see

  • State chips: waiting, held, withheld, failed and, if you ask, what went out. The default view is everything that has not gone out yet.
  • Reason chips with counts, for example "Held by your rules (12)", "Switched off by the administrator (30)", "Waiting for you (4)", "Send failed (2)". Tap one to list only those acts.
  • Period and channel filters. The period goes back at most 90 days.
  • A row shows the kind of act, the channel, the time, the state and the reason. Under the reason, What would change this tells you what to do, if anything. Open the record goes to the post, the reply, the partner pitch or the lead. What was checked opens the record of the check against your rules.

The reasons

Reason What it means
Waiting for you It waits for your approval in Approvals.
Held by your rules Your rules held it until a person confirms it.
Blocked by your rules Your rules blocked it.
Switched off by the administrator Publishing, or this channel, is switched off on the server.
Not allowed here Your account is not on the server's allowlist for this channel.
Channel turned off by you You turned the channel off in Settings › Integrations.
Not connected The account, token or mailbox is not connected.
No way to send yet PULSE has no way to send to this channel; download the content instead.
Sender details missing Your sender name, postal address or unsubscribe link is missing.
Daily limit reached The daily limit of emails, replies or calls was reached.
Unsubscribed The person unsubscribed or is on your do-not-contact list.
Reply window closed The platform only lets a business answer within a time that has passed.
Waiting for its time, hours or media It is scheduled, outside working hours, or needs a photo or video.
Missed its time, approval lapsed Its time passed before it was approved, or its approval expired.
Send failed The platform or the mailbox refused it, or the send failed.
Other reason PULSE did not record a more specific reason; open the record.

"What would change this" is information only. PULSE never changes a switch, a rule or an allowlist for you. When the answer is "ask your administrator", the administrator decides.

What is not listed

  • Acts blocked before PULSE recorded them. If a loop never started because a switch was off, or a call was refused on the screen before your rules were asked, nothing was written down, so nothing can be listed. The page says so.
  • Anything older than 90 days, and traces older than 30 days (that is how long the record of a check is kept). A row older than that has no What was checked link.
  • A very busy account shows the newest acts of each kind; the page names the kind it cut.

When PULSE could not read one kind of record, the page says "Not measured" and names it. It never counts it as zero.

Privacy

A row is an id, a kind, a channel, a time, a state, a reason code and links. No message text, no address and no name leaves the record. The list is yours alone: every signed-in role from viewer up can read it for their own account, and service accounts and automations read nothing. It reads what PULSE already keeps, writes nothing and adds no new storage.
