Voice-overs in your own voice

Voice-overs in your own voice

By default a voice-over is spoken by one of the platform's built-in voices. Voice-overs in your own voice is a separate option that is switched off until you, your administrator and the voice service have each allowed it. The voice is your own, made from the recording you added with your consent in Digital twins: nothing else can be uploaded here, and no other person's voice is accepted anywhere.

What has to be on

  1. Your administrator has switched on PULSE_OWN_VOICE for the server.
  2. The voice service has recorded voices turned on. This is a setting of the platform's voice service, off by default, and it uses more memory and the server's graphics card. If it is off, the option reads Not available on this server.
  3. You have switched on Settings > Account > My own voice.
  4. You have a recording of your own voice with a recorded consent, and the consent has not been withdrawn.

Make one

In the Voice-over studio, the My own voice card shows under the voice choices when it is switched on. Choose Use my own voice, then tick the confirmation each time: "This voice is yours. You confirm you are the speaker." Only a count of confirmations is kept, never your words. Then choose Make in my voice.

Limits

  • A script in your own voice is at most 3,000 characters, because making a voice from a recording is slow and uses the server's graphics card.
  • Up to 5 voice-overs a day in your own voice (PULSE_OWN_VOICE_DAILY_CAP). They also count toward the 20 voice-overs a day of the studio.
  • One at a time for each account.
  • A script that speaks as another real person, that tries to persuade on politics, or that gives medical or legal advice or claims is refused with a reason, and the refused text is not kept.
  • Your own voice is never used for phone calls.

What it is called

The audio is a synthetic voice made from your recording. It is marked "AI-generated voice (your voice)" on the screen, and the file carries "AI-generated voice" and "voice: own (synthetic)". PULSE does not add a watermark, and does not say the voice cannot be told from yours.

Your recording and withdrawing

Your recording stays sealed in your own storage. For each voice-over PULSE gives it to the voice service for that one job and asks the service to forget the voice when the job ends, whether it worked, failed or was stopped.

Withdrawing the consent for the recording, deleting the recording or the twin, or deleting your account stops a voice-over being made and deletes the voice-overs made in that voice, with their audio files, the same way the videos made from your recordings are deleted. The confirmation says so. Voice-overs made with a built-in voice are not touched.
