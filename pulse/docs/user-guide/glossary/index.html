Glossary

Glossary

The words the app uses, in alphabetical order.

Word Meaning
Active Your switch on a channel's card in Settings → Integrations. An Active channel gets PULSE's daily drafts, is planned in campaigns, and may publish.
Approve Your OK on a draft. It hands the post to the schedule; it does not by itself publish. See Drafts, approval and publishing.
Armed / disarmed An automation is disarmed until you arm it: until then it runs only as a dry run. Your administrator's publishing switches are armed or disarmed in the same sense.
Automate The button on every card that opens that item's own automation: a trigger, then steps.
Autopilot PULSE posting what you approved at each account's best time, and drafting replies to comments. Per account, in Settings → Integrations.
Best time When your posts get the most reactions and comments, measured from your own posts once enough of them have results. Until then PULSE uses a starting schedule and says so.
Brand Analysis The landing page: your check-up, what PULSE did, and your results.
Campaign A goal with dates, channels and a plan of posts. Its own page is /campaigns/<id>.
Check-up One run of PULSE over your website and the web: site score, AI and search visibility, leads, competitors, partners, content ideas. Also called a site audit.
Checks The tests PULSE runs on a draft it wrote (opening line, caption, slides, hashtags …): passed, needs work, or could not be checked.
Content ideas Post ideas for one kind of customer, one problem and one angle, backed by what the check-up measured.
Counted A figure from PULSE's own records. A 0 that is counted is a real 0.
Draft A written post waiting for your approval (column Drafts to approve). Also the button that writes a message to a lead.
Dry run Running an automation to see what it would do, without doing it.
Held back (withheld) A post, reply or proposal that was stopped on purpose: by a publishing switch, a missing connection, or the plan gate. It is never sent later without you.
Integrations The Settings tab where you connect accounts and switch channels on.
Lead A person or business that could buy from you, on the Leads board.
Measured / Not measured Measured: a real source answered. Not measured: no source did, so no figure is shown. Never a 0. See Measured and not measured.
Missed A post whose time passed while it waited for approval. It is never sent late.
Needs a connection Only a connected account can answer this.
Needs you now What waits for you: plan tasks, drafts, replies (on Brand Analysis) or posts (on Campaigns).
Not recorded It happened, but PULSE kept no record of it.
Partner A business, association, newsletter, creator or show you could work with. On Leads → Partners.
Persona A kind of customer you talk to, taken from your check-up and your leads, never invented. See Personas.
Plan Your plan (site fixes and the 30-day follow-up cycle) on Brand Analysis; or a campaign's plan of phases and posts.
Plan gate PULSE's reviewer: it checks what PULSE proposes against the evidence before it is saved. Also called "PULSE's reviewer" in the app. See The plan gate.
Planned A post a campaign's plan holds that is not written yet.
Proposed by PULSE A campaign PULSE suggests from your check-up. It is read only until you accept it.
Public tender A public tender or request for proposals that matches what you sell: a card on the Leads board with a Public tender chip.
Scheduled Approved, with a time to go out.
Sent It really went out. Nothing reaches Sent by hand.
Skill The way PULSE writes one kind of post (an Instagram Reel, a LinkedIn post …): instructions, then the checks.
Step trail ("Its steps") A post's history: planned, drafted, approved, scheduled, then sent, withheld, failed or missed.
Studio Where content is made (Make something new) and kept (My Library).

Pages and words PULSE no longer uses, and where each went, are on Old names and addresses.
