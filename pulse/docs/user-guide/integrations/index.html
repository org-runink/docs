      User guide
      Settings → Integrations

      Connect your accounts

      Settings → Integrations (/settings?tab=integrations) is one grid of cards, one per account or channel, in two sections:

      • Publish to: Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Website, Website repository, Podcast, Audiobook, and Email (your mailbox).
      • Measure & CRM: Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Search Console, HubSpot, SAM.gov, and the Website form.

      Below the grid is Autopilot (see Autopilot and agencies).

      This page is the owner’s side: what you click and what you get. Some connections first need your administrator to register PULSE with the platform (an app in Meta’s, Google’s or LinkedIn’s developer console). When that is missing, the card says so and names what the administrator must set. The administrator’s side is in Connections and Switches and arming order.

      PULSE keeps every token and password encrypted, and never shows a pasted one again.

      What a “Publish to” card tells you

      • Active: your switch. An Active channel gets PULSE’s daily drafts, is planned in your campaigns, and may publish. Turning it off stops all three. Save after changing it.
      • A one-line status: Ready to publish, or Can’t publish: and the reason in plain words, for example Connect an account first., Publishing is switched off for this whole deployment. Posts are kept and marked held back., or Only this deployment’s named publishers can post here.
      • Recent dispatches: what was posted, held back or failed, with the date.

      Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn

      Connect opens the platform’s own sign-in in a new browser tab. Sign in, allow what PULSE asks for, then come back. (If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for PULSE.)

      Once connected, the card shows:

      • Signed in, and when the sign-in ends. PULSE renews this sign-in before it ends where the platform allows it; otherwise it says Reconnect before that date. A failed renewal is shown with its date and reason.
      • What each feature has: Publishing, Results, Comments and Direct messages, each allowed, needs a named permission, not checked, or not offered by that platform to apps (YouTube has no mentions and no direct messages for apps).
      • Reconnect asks the platform again, for example to allow a permission you declined. Disconnect removes PULSE’s access at the platform where it can and deletes the stored sign-in; your settings for the account are kept.

      If your administrator has not set up sign-in for a platform, the card says … sign-in isn’t set up on this server and offers to paste a token instead. A pasted token works, but PULSE can neither check its permissions nor renew it, and the card says so.

      PlatformWhat you connectWhat PULSE does with it
      InstagramA professional (business or creator) Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page, through Facebook LoginPublishes posts, carousels, Reels and Stories; reads results, comments, mentions and direct messages for Autopilot
      YouTubeYour Google account and its YouTube channelUploads Shorts; reads views, likes, comments and watch time
      LinkedInYour LinkedIn member accountPosts as you. Connect company pages (when your administrator has enabled it) lets you choose a company page you administer as the author instead

      Google Search Console

      Connect Search Console signs in with Google, read-only: PULSE never changes anything in Search Console. Then Choose the site PULSE reads; the one that matches the site you audited is marked. PULSE reads it once a day: the clicks, appearances, click rate and position of each search, for the last 28 days and the 28 before (Google’s figures run about two days behind).

      The card says when it last read and up to which day, or why the last read failed. Read now, Change site and Disconnect are on it. If your Google account has no verified site, add your site at search.google.com/search-console first, then reconnect.

      What it fills: Can people find you? on Brand Analysis, the Search clicks and Search impressions lines, and the demand behind content ideas and campaign plans (“people searched X; your site ranks 11”).

      Google Analytics and Google Ads

      These are connected with a key you create in Google Cloud and paste on the card:

      • Google Analytics: the JSON key of a service account with access to your GA4 property, and the numeric Property ID (GA4 Admin → Property Settings).
      • Google Ads: the JSON key of a service account with domain-wide delegation, your Ads Customer ID, a Developer Token, optionally a Login Customer ID (manager account), and the user email to act as.

      Test Connection checks them. They fill the visits, page views, ad views, ad clicks and spend on Brand Analysis and in reports. If this is not something you do yourself, ask whoever manages your Google Cloud; the details are in Connections.

      Your website

      There are two cards, for two jobs:

      • Website publishes your approved blog articles to your site through GitHub. Paste a GitHub token that can push to your site’s repository. Which repository it publishes to is set by your administrator.
      • Website repository is where PULSE proposes fixes to your site as pull requests. See below.

      Website repository

      Fill in the Owner (your GitHub user or organization), the Repository name, the Base branch, and a Fine-grained access token: create it on GitHub under Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens, for this one repository only, with Contents and Pull requests set to Read and write. Then Connect and check. The card says Connected, or what is wrong: Missing permission, Token rejected, Base branch not found or GitHub unreachable. Check again re-checks it.

      From then on, a fix the site score found can be proposed with Propose as pull request on the site-score report. PULSE writes the change on a new branch and opens a pull request for you to review and merge. PULSE never merges and never changes your base branch. Each fix shows its pull request’s state as last read from GitHub: Pull request open, Merged or Closed without merging, or No pull request needed, or Withheld by the plan gate when PULSE’s reviewer did not agree with the change.

      Proposing needs an administrator account, and your administrator must have switched on publishing and site fixes on this PULSE. Until then the button is refused with the reason.

      Podcast and audiobook

      Nothing to connect: PULSE publishes these itself, in its own podcast feed that Spotify and Apple read. Turn them Active to use them.

      Email

      Connect your own mailbox, so that Send on a lead’s email sends from your address: Connect Gmail, Connect Microsoft 365, or an SMTP server. With Gmail or Microsoft 365, PULSE also sees who replied. See Leads → Email.

      HubSpot

      Paste the access token of a HubSpot Private App (in HubSpot: Settings → Integrations → Private Apps) with the CRM contacts permissions. Then Sync from HubSpot on Leads imports your contacts, new leads PULSE finds are added to HubSpot, and your website form’s enquiries go there too.

      SAM.gov

      For US federal tenders on Opportunities: paste your SAM.gov public API key (on sam.gov: your profile, Account Details, API key). PULSE only reads opportunities with it.

      Website form

      Turns your site’s contact form into leads. See Website form.
