PULSE Documentation

PULSE Documentation

Runink PULSE: Prescriptive Unified Lead & Social Engine is a marketing agency in an app: it checks how your business looks online, plans campaigns, writes and makes the posts, and publishes them only after you approve, on models that run on Runink's own platform.

Our own models · no third-party AI

Your marketing agency in an app

PULSE checks how your business looks online, plans campaigns, writes and makes the posts, and publishes them only once you approve.

Get started

  • Brand Analysis: One check-up of your site and your market: your score, the fixes to make, who could buy from you, who you compete with and what people search for.
  • Campaigns: Every campaign on one board of action cards, from planned to sent, with each post's steps traced on its campaign's page.
  • Studio: Posts, carousels, Instagram Reels and Stories, YouTube Shorts and documents in your brand colours, checked before you see them, ready to download or publish.
  • Leads and public tenders: People who could buy from you and public tenders that ask for websites, apps and content, on one board.
  • Measured, never made up: Every figure comes from a source PULSE read. What it could not read says “not measured”, never 0.
  • Connect your accounts: Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Search Console, Google Analytics and Ads, and your website's repository, from Settings.
  • Run and configure: Setup for each connector, the publishing switches and their arming order, and the full API.
